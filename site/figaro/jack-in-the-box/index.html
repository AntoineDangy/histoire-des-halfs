    Octobre "Seahorse", OUFTI 40 - Mach 40. Price: 137 000 EUR. Located in Saint-Malo, The Class40 OUFTI 40 is an offshore racing yacht designed by renowned naval architect Sam Manuard and built in 2011 by the reputable shipyard JPS Production. This Mach 40 was upgraded in 2018 to the V2 configuration, including major improvements to the structure, rigging, and ballast systems. Since then, it has benefited from a continuous optimisation programme. Fully compliant with the latest Class40 regulations, including 2024 standards, it offers a competitive and reliable racing platform.
