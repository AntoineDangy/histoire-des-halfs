    26 novembre Classe Figaro website, Charter a Figaro 3 for the 2020 season. The racing programme is rich with two-handed and singlehanded events in 2020. The AG2R is a two-handed transatlantic race that starts from Concarneau in April and finishes in beautiful St Barths. There are several singlehanded events early in the season and then a couple more during the summer months before the mighty Solitaire du Figaro in late August and September. Come and try this exceptional class of strict One Design offshore racing. The competition standard is high, the circuit is exciting and the friendships are for life.... Figaro Number 38 is available for charter for part or all of the season. It is available to charter with or without sails, is equipped to the highest One Design Class Rules standard, and will be delivered with fresh antifouling and fully up to date with the 2020 class rules. The charter deal includes the use of a workshop-equipped and storage 40-foot container in Port La Foret. English mother tongue coaching and project management is also available. Call or email for more details or discuss your project.
