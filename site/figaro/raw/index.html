    11 juin, "Former Irish Sailor of the Year, Conor Fogarty arrived onto the dock in Dun Laoghaire from Les Sables de Olinges with “Raw”, a fresh out of the box, foiling Beneteau Figaro 3, Ireland’s first IRC foiling Figaro keelboat."

    12 juin, The Volvo Dun Laoghaire to Dingle Race: 18e/45 IRC elapsed time: 1d11h00', co-skipper Susan Glenny, Conor FOGERTY

    June "Seahorse", For Rent, Figaro 3 -Raw, Available for: UK 2019 Day Sail, Caribbean 2020, The Figaro 3 is the first production foiling one-design monohull. Resulting from a collaboration between group Beneteau’s best experts and the Van Peteghem Lauriot-Prévost (VPLP) office, the architects of the two last boats to win the Vendée Globe, this boat is fast, powerful and very exciting to sail. It is equipped with foils, a ballast-free hull, a slender and deeper keel, a setback mast and an extensive and large sail plan. The Figaro 3 has recently been awarded Sailing Worlds “Boat of the Year” for 2019, with judges praising its sailing on all points of sail! Chartering the Figaro 3 will be the experience of a lifetime. A truly awesome racing machine, you are bound to have an exhilarating time on the water putting the boat through its paces.
