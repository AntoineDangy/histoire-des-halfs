    Octobre, Rare - Fully optimised Figaro2 maintained to the highest standards. Fantastic offshore race yacht fully configured and ready for a full solo / shorthanded campaign. No52 - GBR 5236R

    Rare achieved 3rd in RORC Two Handed Class for the 2014 season. It recently succesfully completed Sevenstar Round Britain and Ireland race achieving line honours for doublehanded and record for the smallest boat ever to complete the race. Fully compliant to ISAF Offshore Category1 including RORC prescriptions. Full refits in 2009 & 2010, dry sailed since and fully maintained by owner and Carbon Yachting in Hamble.

    Extensive sail wardrobe for both Figaro and IRC configurations. Brand new 3DL North Main and A2 in Aug 2014. IRC carbon sprit configuration can be removed with 4 bolts and restored to full Figaro class spec. Comprehensive electronics package - NKE including spares, Full safety equipment including Liferaft. Extensive spares and cradle. A fantastic offshore race yacht ready for a Transat, a full Figaro or shorthanded sailing season with RORC, JOG, RSYC and or solo campaign with SORC. Please Contact: Ian Hoddle
