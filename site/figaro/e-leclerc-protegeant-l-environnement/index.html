    SUNY Maritime College, This boat was re-named SPIRIT by her two private donors, both CCA members, who combined their donations to fund this boat and their thoughts to name her. She comes from a private owner and competitor from St. Malo on the north coast of France, the home of the legendary Route du Rhum race. She raced extensively in the intense French Figaro circuit, mostly recently under the names Loupiot and Apache(?!).
