  3. 1999 Victoire

    mT: 34e préinscrit, puis INSCRIT No 39, Peter HEPPEL, 44 ans.

    330'CC: 2e/40 à 4h. (trou de vent à l'arrivée!) à mi-parcours, à 3' de Magnen. "j'ai beaucoup appris!"

  12. 2009

    28 mars, mise à l'eau à Cowes.

    23 juin, Ben's Solo Atlantic Run Complete, The target - to take a peak over the Continental Shelf into really deep water at 15 Degrees West somewhere south west of Ireland. This translates into a solo unassisted round trip of at least 1,100 nautical miles +; 7 - 9 days at sea in a 6.50m racing yacht that has less by way of comforts than any Open 60 or VO70.

    10 juillet,Round Britain Island singlehanded: 15j03h08' Ben ROGERSON

    Ben Rogerson and his Mini-Transat 6.50m yacht ’Mini Minx’ triumphant with successful completion of 1,924 Nautical Mile Solo Non-Stop Round Britain & Ireland Passage at 0625 hrs UTC Saturday 25 July 2009 in a total of 15 days 3 hours and 8 minutes. This is the first Mini-Transat 6.50m to achieve this feat and we believe the first boat of this size of any design to do so.
