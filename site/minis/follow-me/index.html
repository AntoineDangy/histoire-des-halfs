  26. 2026 Refit, modification

    13 avril, vu dans un chantier à Hennebont

    5 mai/15 juillet site Class Mini, Mini 6.50 #759 – Ashika II: Refitted & Race Ready, Originally built by Milan Kolácek (Sam Manuard design), Ashika II is a high-performance Mini 6.50 fully optimized for solo offshore racing. Following a major 2023 Seascape refit (spatula bow, carbon hull rebuild), the boat underwent a second comprehensive refit in 2025. This included full maintenance and structural resolution of the autopilot issues experienced during the Mini Transat 2025. Equipped with brand-new 2025 North Sails (Mainsail, Jib, A2), high-end NKE electronics, and a Watt&Sea hydrogenerator, she is 100% compliant with Class Mini Category A requirements. 55k€
