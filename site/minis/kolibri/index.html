    19 septembre Site Class Mini, For Sale Dingo 2 Series with V2 rig 746 “Husky”, ex. “Kolibri”, Boat launched 2008/2009. One Transat in year 2011, many Podiums since. With road trailer, HUSKY is in very good condition, is a very reliable and robust Mini. It has been retrofitted with a new (Classemini approved) rig with wide chain plates and boomerang-style lower spreaders in 2013, replacing the early narrow chainplate base. A Dingo 2 is also very spacious below deck and has great cockpit ergonomics. Location: Summer: Kiel/Germany Winter: Hamburg/Germany
