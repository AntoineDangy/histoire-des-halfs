  5. 2012 Mise en vente

    2 juillet, For Sale TipTop Series Mini, 65 000 USD, Great opportunity to jump into a well prepared and proven mini in the US. This boat has done the Bermuda1-2 twice, in 2009 it was the fastest Series Mini and in 2011 it was the fastest mini outright and the boat currently holds the records for fastest series mini (97 hours in 2011). The boat comes with just about everything needed to race, including full NKE pack, IQ90 solar panel on carbon mount, dual axis trailer, new sails for last B12. SA thread from last year's fun, More information, images, and videos about Frogger. Located: Long Island, NY
