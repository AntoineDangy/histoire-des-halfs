    Juin, site Mini, The RG-650 is the newest member of the Mini 6.50 production class family. TEAM WICHARD is a prime example of why the RG 650 is the fastest Mini on the water with her winning pedigree in races like the Bermuda 1-2. Built in Argentina by a specialized team and under a strict quality control, the RG 650 is a One Design ocean racing boat; as established by the Classe Mini. TEAM WICHARD is set up as a PROTO/OPEN classe Mini. This boat has a fully optimizes rig and sails plan to take every advantage of rule allowances. One of the most highly optimized mini's there is!
