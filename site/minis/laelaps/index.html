Chronologie détaillée

  1. 2004

    Septembre, "Somewhere in the woods of the United States a sailor is preparing his Zero for next racing season".

  2. 2006

    Triangle du Soleil: 11e et dernier/25 ttc. Éq: Alexandre VERDYS, Andy ABEL

  3. 2006
    Baptisé Laelaps

    3-4 juin, Mini Solo: 31e/35ttc, 15e/17 série à 1h10'.

  4. 2006 Abandon de course
    Devient Zonda

    2-7 juillet, MiniMax: 34e/42 ttc en 4j13h, 16e/21 série, à 1j7h. Éq: Alexandre VERDYS

    Aout, Les Sables-Açores: dnf - dns = DNC/69ttc.

    Andy Abel a fini sur les cayoux de Pico (Açores). Il n’a pu éviter les dangers, il n'avait plus qu'un safran, le skipper est sain et sauf.

    Le bateau est déclaré épave.

  5. 2013
    Devient Ilhéu Deitado POR 632

    4 mars,from João Nunes: "Ilhéu Deitado", antigo "Zonda", foi reconstruído pelo seu proprietário Nuno Santos, e após 6 anos de estaleiro foi para o mar hoje, dia 04 de março de 2013.

    The boat 632 was recovered and is on the water after 6 years, Now owned by Nuno Santos from Horta.

  6. 2015 VictoireMise en vente

    24 July, Atlantis Cup: 7 - 7 - 1 = 4e/6 Open, Nuno SANTOS

    9 August, Regata do Canal, Horta: 1er/12, temps réel: 1h35'

    August, Pictures and information received from João Nunes

    November, Zero 632 for sale, 1 main sail, 1genaker, 2 jibs, 3 spinnakers, Raymarine electronics, Almost ready for mini transat, located in Azores

  7. 2017 Vendu, nouveau propriétaire

    New owner Francisco Sousa based in Azores, Terceira Island

  8. 2020

    New paint job

  9. 2021

    August, last update and picture received from Francisco,
