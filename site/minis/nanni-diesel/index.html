    October, site class mini, For Sale Atomic 56 is a mini prototype that in recent years has been sailed in Swedish waters, both racing (previous owner) and cruising. Right now the boat is on the west coast and in a few weeks it will go to the winter storage (indoors) I have sailed Atomic with joy, it planes away quite easily and in downwind with some waves it is a pure pleasure to sail. The boat is well equipped and has had continuous maintenance and updates. Most recently, the keel was renovated and the deck was painted. In addition, lots of small details have been fixed. Thus, it is ready to sail from day 1, 12000,- Euro
