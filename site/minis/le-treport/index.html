    3 septembre Class Mini Website, Rackham Le Rouge is for sale. 2004 Ginto series boat, designed by Seb Magnen, designer of 2-time MiniTransat winner Karen Liquid (198). Simple, reliable boat, may not win the next Minitransat but everything works well. V lightly sailed since 2011. Much new kit over the last 3 years. Affordable introduction to the class, boat in the UK (Solent). Price guide £15-£20k, dependent on equipment included in the sale, but open to all sensible discussions. Much loved boat, but growing family request more accommodation!
