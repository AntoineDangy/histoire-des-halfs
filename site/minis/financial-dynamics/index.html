  11. 2003 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en venteRefit, modification

    Aout, Proto 116 for sale. "Soleil du Sud", originally "Le Poisson" and "Wild Colonial Boy" Jaques Faroux design, first of the canting keel boats. I bought from Ellen Macarthur for Nick Moloney to use in '99. Came to Australia in 2000. In 99 had new mast and centre board fitted. In 2002 had new carbon keel with bulb fitted, carbon rudders and carbon bowsprit. All wiring has been stripped and reinstalled to be submergable! Currently in Melbourne, Australia. 30,000 euro (will arrange shipping to europe if required)

    AUS 116, Mini Bar,
