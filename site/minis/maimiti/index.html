  13. 2024 Mise en vente

    February, "Rogue" is For Sale. Luther and I have had a blast owning this boat, it is time for someone else to have some fun with it. Here is a description of the boat: Prototype Mini Transat 6.50 Marc Lombard Designed Built in 1988/89 Draft 6' 6" LOA 21' Beam 9' 6" Mast Height 49'6" Bow Sprit 9'10" Completed the Mini Transat Race 4x Carbon Fiber Foam Sandwich Construction. Canting Keel with twin daggerboards and twin rudders. Full restoration completed spring of 2020 Dry sailed 4 year old UK Sails inventory, includes full batten Dacron Class Main with three reef points. 1 UK Jib carbon Tafetta with 1 reef point. A2 Spinnaker, A5 Spinnaker, Code Zero with Ronstan Furler and torsion cable. Misc. Older yet in great shape sails. Storm Trisail, Storm Jib, A2, A5 and 2 S2 spinnakers. Pineapple Sails Jib. Raymarine electronics package, wind, depth, speed and tillerpilot with remote. 100 watt solar panel with charge controller. 2 group 24 deep cycle batteries with victron battery monitor. Custom from the factory built Torqeedo electric outboard with two batteries. Carbon Fiber Mast, Boom and bow Sprit. LS Rigging carbon fiber headstay (New 2020) Dyneema Dux standing rigging All running rigging and deck hardware brand new in 2020 Double Axel trailer This is a very unique boat that is very fast and ready to do some short handed offshore racing. $40,000 USD obo. Boat is located in Berkeley, CA
