Chronologie détaillée

  1. 2015 Construction, mise à l’eau

    Plan Bertrand, Diamand 6.50

  2. 2015

    August, Baltic Polonez Cup 2015, Poland: 2e/21 Open 2 double, Marek Skorwider & Jacek Sliwinski

    September, Bitwa O Gotland, 468NM: ?e/?? Open, Krzysztof KOLAKOWSKI

  3. 2019 Mise en vente

    23 juin Site Class Mini, A Vendre - For Sale, Boat no: 884, ARAO, Built: 2015, Design Étienne Bertrand, Boat very similar to OFCET 650, but 100% vacuum infused, structural frames each infused in separate mold, no cut out of shapes, no wood used at any point/element, Boat weight :970 kg (including 430 kg bulb keel), Full electrocics pack Raymarine, Evolution 200 Autopilot with ACU-200, Mainsail- 2017, Triradial, Dimension - Polyant PX Black Line, Jib - 2018, Triradial, Dimension - Polyant PX Black Line, Medium genaker, Code 0 on carver roller, Boat price: 27,000 Eur, Trailer price: 2,000 Eur

  4. 2020 VictoireVendu, nouveau propriétaire
    Devient Libera CRO 884

    New owner, Croatian olympionic skier and professional sailor Ivica Kostelic and used both for training (in view of a possible participation in the Olympics when it was still assumed to include offshore sailing)

    10 juillet, La Duocento, 155 NM: 3e/4 Minis, Ivica KOSTELIC

    5 septembre, La Cinquecento, 500 NM: 1er/1 Minis

    25 september, Regata Trieste/San Giovanni in Pelago (84NM) = 2e/8 Minis elapsed time 8h34'

  5. 2021 Vendu, nouveau propriétaire

    November, new owner, Roberto Ghini

    December, Pictures and informations received from Roberto, "the plan is to participate both in the Italian championship and in offshore regattas in the Adriatic. The boat is in very good conditions. Light and fast."
