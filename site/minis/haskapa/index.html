  6. 2014 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en vente

    Proto 713 seriously for sale. Asking £10,000. Currently located in Antigua and just completed solo transatlantic voyage from Gibraltar to Antigua (see www.tropic4cancer.com).

    Can organise shipping to Europe if required (cost approx £6000). Built 2008 by Race2Win in Germany. Fixed keel, aluminium mast. In good condition, with lots of new equipment.

    1st of July, Haskapa has been sold to Justin Hickey, an Australian sailor, who is using her as part of his training programme for a 2016 Vendee Globe bid
