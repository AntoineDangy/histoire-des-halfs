  1. 2014 Refit, modificationConstruction, mise à l’eau

    Plan Manuard, Mach 40 construit par JPS Production à la Trinité.

    Stella Nova is boat #4 and has some interesting upgrades to the older generations including more rake in the rig, modifications in the ballast and the keel raked two degrees aft.
