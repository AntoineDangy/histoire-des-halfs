  10. 2022 Mise en vente
    Devient Cefalo 7 Spirit Of ITA 86

    August, A Vendre - For Sale Pogo 40 Racing version, Voilier de croisière/régate de l'année 2008 de 12,1m de longueur à Porto di Lavagna, Ligurie (Italie), 120 000 Euros, The class 40 S/Y ¿Cefalo 7 Spirit Of¿ is a Pogo 40 of the first generation, build by Structures Shipyard with the objective to offer a racing Class 40 Boat but also a fast cruising S/Y with accommodations for increasing the level of comfort.This specific boat, number 86, has never been in the ocean and the racing history has been very limited also considering that the first owner was a 70yeas old sailor.She is a well-proven, reliable and seaworthy boat that has been well maintained and extremely well equipped.She has been fitted out with a comfortable interior and is also adapted to single handed long distance cruising.
