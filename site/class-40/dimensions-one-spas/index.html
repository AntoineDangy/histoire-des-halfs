  18. 2007 Vendu, nouveau propriétaire

    Class 40 Akilaria RC1, 115 000 €, Halmstad, Suède. A solid, 1st generation dual purpose Class 40 Akilaria RC1 Letto de Pletto #47 is a racer/cruiser in good condition. Originally raced short-handed in Class 40 races, she has most recently been raced in Sweden with a full crew. Her latest owners have kept her well maintained with multiple upgrades to ensure she has many more miles of performance racing or cruising for her new owner. The Marc Lombard designed Class 40 Akilaria RC1 Letto de Pletto #47 was built of sandwich construction with vinylester resin by MC-TEC and launched in 2007. She comes with an extensive inventory included in the price, she is priced to sell and will be ideal for either vintage category Class 40 racing, long offshore races or fast blue water cruising. An early viewing of Class 40 Akilaria RC1 Letto de Pletto #47 is highly recommended.
