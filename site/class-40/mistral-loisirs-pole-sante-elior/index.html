    30 janvier, le bateau est ramené à Falmouth, info et photo de Facebook,

    6 mars, Class 40 Akilaria Rc2 For Sale, New to the market and with motivated sellers this vessel needs to be seriously considered by anyone wanting a project class 40 that could provide a huge amount of fun. Obviously to get the boat up and sailing again will require some considerable work and enough funds but in the right hands this could be carried out cost effectively. Class 40#83 was involved in a collision at sea whilst racing in the 2018 Route du Rhum, with poor conditions it was decided to evacuate the skipper and the boat was left to fend for herself. The tracker was switched off and the vessel was believed lost until she was later found and subsequently recovered. She has been evaluated by insurers surveyors and much of her useful loose equipment has been stored. The boat is being sold as seen with no warranties given or implied, any potential purchaser should evaluate the vessel themselves as to its condition and potential.
