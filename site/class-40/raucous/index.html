  4. 2009 Mise en venteConstruction, mise à l’eau

    For sale - A Vendre, Open 40 - Finot Conq Hull No.2, This yacht is a water ballasted, lifting keel sister-ship of Anasazi Girl which has been privately owned and sailed in the Mediterranean most of its life. She was beautifully built in Spain by SAM C3 and then fitted out by the well known Vendee preparation organisation V1D2. Since then the yacht has been professionally managed and maintained and is in excellent condition, ready to race or fast cruise. Designer: Finot Conq Sails: Original Incidences inventory, low mileage compared to contemporaries; Builder: Sam C3 and V1D2, Materials: Carbon pre-preg monolithic hull and Nomex sandwich deck; Launched: 2003; Draft: Max 3.20m, Min 2.04m; Beam: 4.10m; Class: Open 40; Ballast: Water ballast – 4 x lateral tanks; Mast: Alucarbon rotating wing section with Navtec Kevlar rigging (2003); Deck gear: Harken, Spinlock, Facnor and Lewmar windlass; Motor: Yanmar 27hp diesel with saildrive and Gori prop; Electronics: B and G Hercules 2000 instrument package including: wind, log, echo sounder and pilots. VHF DSC radio, Furuno radar, computers, GPS, navigation lights, compass, etc. Safety equipment: Includes two EPIRBS, liferaft, MOB units, flares, two survival suits and personal safety gear for six persons. Accommodation: 1 double, six single berths, cooker, fridge, heads with shower and hot/cold pressurised water. All sails from Incidences 2003, Miscellaneous: Dinghy included in inventory; Registered: France Lying: Southern France, Asking Price: 350,000 Euro (VAT Paid)

  7. 2019

    2019 Février, Beautifully built, finished and equipped to the highest standard in 2003. This is the boat that developed into the Pogo 40 Class 40. A truly magnificent beast that has few equals even today, with her advanced composite construction, carbon lifting keel fin system by CARIBONI, a super efficient wingmast and an interior fitout that works equally well in harbour or out on the open ocean. Little used and professionally maintained and constantly upgraded. This is a genuinely cruiseable yet high performance sailing yacht with large double berth forward, dedicated heads compartment with sea toilet sink and shower. Further aft is the galley opposite the lifting keel box. Dedicated navigation station to port. Spacious saloon seating area amidships and 4 x quarter berths aft under the cockpit complete the interior layout. She has 4 x watertight bulkheads and is certified Category A. Designer: Finot-Conq, Builder: Sam-C3 Spain / V1D2 France, Launched: July 2003, Location: Italy, Price: 195,000 Euro, VAT Status: Paid
