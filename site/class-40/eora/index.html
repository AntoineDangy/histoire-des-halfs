  5. 2024

    14 septembre, Caen, photos reçues de Alexandre,

    15 septembre, Normandy Channel Race, Caen, 1000 NM: 7e/25 Class 40 en 5j00h29' à 3h12', éq: Corentin DOUGUET, Henry RUPERT

    November "Seahorse", Class 40 #169 'Eora'. PRICE 650,000 ex-VAT EUR. Located in La Trinite, France, Eora is a fantastic example of the latest design from Marc Lombard, a Lift V2 Class 40, built with Glass Epoxy Sandwich by Innovation Composites and launched in 2021. In her short career in 2022, she placed 6 th in the Normandy Channel Race while the Lift V2 design is at the top of the game with results such as: 2022 Route du Rhum 1 st and 3 rd , 2022 Drheam Cup 2 nd and 2022 Normandy Channel Race 2 nd . With the proven success of the scow bow this boat has been designed to perform fast and efficiently on all points of sail. She is a fast and beautiful boat in excellent sail away condition.
