  3. 2010 AvarieGrande course
    Baptisé Dms

    31 octobre, Route du Rhum: 39e/85ttc en 20j04h56', 14e/44 Class 40 à 2j05h46', vitesse moyenne: 7,30nds, Pete GOSS

    "C'était génial, j'ai vraiment apprécié. Je recommande la Route du Rhum à tout le monde ! C'était fantastique, j'ai pris du plaisir et ne déplore aucune avarie, le bateau est impeccable. Je n'avais pas navigué en solitaire depuis 13 ans et cette course et après les quatre premiers jours, j'ai vraiment pu trouver mon rythme et de bons réflexes. Je trouve le concept de la Class40 très bon, les courses sont fabuleuses avec un bon niveau de compétition, et les bateaux vraiment excitants. Cette jeune classe va décoller, j'en suis sûr."

  14. 2019 Mise en vente

    February, Seen on AZAB Entrants list, Simon Costain

    30 May, Class 40 website, For Sale-A Vendre #93 Concice 2

    1 June, AZAB: DNS

    Class 40 #93 Concise 2. For Sale - A Vendre, This Akilaria RC2 class 40 has been very successful, winning the Round Britain and Ireland race; Round Island Race; Normandy channel race; 2011 RORC Caribbean 600 race. The yacht has a proven pedigree of terrific seaworthiness and performance as it has been raced across the Atlantic several times by Pete Goss in the La Route Du Rhum race singlehanded; and by Sam Goodchild and Ned Collier Wakefield in the RBI race/ Normandy Chanel race. The boat was fully surveyed in late 2017 and again in early 2019 and the surveys have shown she is a still a very sound vessel suited for racing at speed either fully crewed or shorthanded. The present owner has refurbished the boat with great care and attention, replacing the engine and gearbox; renewing all NKE electronics; renewing the standing rigging. The boat is still very well-known, the vinyl wrap very striking, competitive and previously has been in the ownership of Team Concise, and many of those sailors said she is still very quick at top level, and ready to race again. She has a large wardrobe of sails and spares too be included in the sale, so is ready to sail/race immediately.
