  16. 2023 Mise en venteRefit, modification

    Owen Clarke Design website, Class 40 - #85 For Sale - A Vendre, he Pogo 40 was designed by renowned architects Finot/Conq. Speed and reliability are just two of the key points when considering one of these vintage category Class 40 designs. Her current owner has completely re-fitted the interior for fast cruising in comfort though all fittings can be removed to allow a return to Class 40 racing. Thanks to an ergonomic cockpit and deck layout, she is simple to sail single handed or with a reduced crew. She is easy to maintain and as with all shorthanded race boats optimised for reliability. This is the ‘S’ model which has the infused composite structure making for a stiffer lighter structure. Ideal as an entry level shorthanded racing yacht or as a very fast cruiser for adventurous families. A great opportunity to pick up a well maintained and fitted out Class 40 Pogo S2 with a good inventory, afloat and ready to go sailing. Lying: Noumea, New Caledonia Built: Structures Designer: Finot Conq Launched: 2010 Price: €130,000 (VAT paid Europe)
