  17. 2023 VictoireMise en vente

    From AZAB race's website "‘La Promesse’, an open 40 built by Phil Morrison will be raced double handed by Wouter Lampe (18) and JanKees Lampe (57). Wouter and JanKees are a father and son who have dreamed about competing together in the AZAB Race. In the summer of 2022, they sailed La Promesse from Newport (RI) to Sao Miguel. La Promesse took line honours on legs in the AZAB 2007 & 2011 races and the OSTAR 2009 and TWOSTAR 2022! They are looking forward to visiting Falmouth again and setting off for another fantastic race!"
