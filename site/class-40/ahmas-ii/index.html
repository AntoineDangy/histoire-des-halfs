  2. 2013 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireConstruction, mise à l’eau

    Maine Yacht Center, Portland Maine has launched the first of two new Akilaria RC3 Class 40’s. MYC has been collaborating with French designer Marc Lombard and Tunisian builder, MC-Tec to import parts for completion at the Portland Maine yard. To date, MYC has imported, completed and sold previous Akilaria versions, RC1 and RC2’s. Mike DREESE
