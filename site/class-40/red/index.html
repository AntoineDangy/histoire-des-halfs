    For Sale 2009 Class 40 Owen Clarke, 130 000 € Toutes Taxes Comprises, Scheveningen, Pays-Bas, The Owen Clarke Class 40 Red #66 was launched in 2009. She was built by Composite Creations using wet-preg epoxy/glass/corecell foam core. She has a fabricated keel fin with a lead bulb, coated with an epoxy paint system before and after being faired. Under her current ownership the Owen Clarke Class 40 Red #66 has been professionally maintained. The Owen Clarke Class 40 Red #66 is a proven and reliable boat. She is sold ‘race ready’ and recently entered the 50th Anniversary Fastnet Race. With some additions she could be considered for Class40 compliance making her a perfect entry level boat. Alternatively, she would make a great candidate for line honours in medium to long offshore races (e.g. AZAB, OSTAR) or with some basic comfort below she is suitable for fast cruising, too.
