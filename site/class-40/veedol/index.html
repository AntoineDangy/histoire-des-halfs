    31 October "Class 40 website", For Sale Polka Dot Class 40, Marc Lombard design was at the start of the scow bow era. Launched in 2018 at V1/D2 shipyard in a collaboration with Gepeto Composites, she was campaigned by Yoann Richomme and won the 2018 Route de Rhum & Drheam Cup. In the following season she went on to achieve 2nd in the RORC Fastnet & Les Sables-Horta races and a 3rd in the Grand Prix Guyader. Polka Dot has been meticulously maintained with a generous investment programme. Safety and reliability have been a key priority.
