Chronologie détaillée

  1. 2001 Construction, mise à l’eau

    Groupe Finot, construit par JMV à Cherbourg

    LOA Feet: 40 Beam: 4,06 Draft: 3,20m Displacement: 3,7t. Coque carbone, quille fixe.

  2. 2001 Construction, mise à l’eau

    Mai, le bateau a été terminé à Caen dans le chantier de Marc Lefèbvre (V1D2). Puis il a été embarqué sur un cargo à Zeebruges pour la Californie, Santa Barbara.

    Fin juin, mise au point avant le départ de la course, avec Jean Marie Finot.

    30 June, Transpacifique Los Angeles-Honolulu, elapsed time 240h49': 4e/5 division 3, Shuichi OGASAWARA

    Le bateau a rejoint le Japon par la mer, comme son programme le prévoit.

  3. 2002 Grande course
    Devient Spirit of Yukoh JPN 11

    2 juin, Kojiro Shiraishi quitte le port de Shimoda au Japon pour se rendre, en solitaire, au départ de la course Around Alone, via le Canal de Panama.

    Around Alone: 5 - 4 - 4 - 3 - 3 = 3e/6, et 1er 40'. Kojiro SHIRAISHI

    1e étape: 5e/6 classe 2, 2e 40'.

    2e étape, fait escale en Espagne lors d'une tempête. 4e/6 classe, 2e 40' en 50j 9h, à18h du 1er 40'.

  4. 2003

    3e étape: 4e/5.

    4e étape: 3e/5.

    5e étape: 3e/5.

    Juin "Course Au Large",

    Anasazi Girl, USA ??

  5. 2005 Mise en vente

    Avril, A Vendre, Architecte : Finot Conq, Chantier : JMV France Tirant d'eau : 3 m, Type : Open 40, construction : Carbone, 2000, Prix demandé : 160 000 USD Moteur : YANMAR, Longueur : 12,18 m, Largeur : 4 m Visible : Est USA

  6. 2006

    Décembre, à Cap Town, Afrique du Sud.

  7. 2007

    16 février, arrivée à Port of Albany, Australie

    Mars-Avril, au Freemantle Sailing Club

    July, à Wellington, Nouvelle Zélande.

    Décembre, à Aukland, Nouvelle Zélande.

  8. 2008

    10 Janvier, aprés 26 jours, passe le Cap Horn.

    Février, 2001 Finot Open 40. US$585,000. Located in Portland, Maine, USA. ANASAZI GIRL was completely renewed and updated 2005 thru 2008. Today the only original components are the hull, deck and keel. Ship's systems have been reengineered, replaced, improved, and upgraded without regard to cost. Only the best technicians in each discipline were utilized. Their guidance provided state-of-art systems of the highest quality. Available for someone who wants the fastest, strongest, safest, proven world passagemaker at a fraction of the cost of replacement.

  9. 2009

    30 mai, received mail from Mr James M Burwick: "I purchased Open 40 Spirit of Yukoh from Koshiro re name ANASAZI GIRL, I make Bermuda/Cape Town/Auckland/Cape Town/Bermuda Solo", www.anasaziracing.org

  10. 2011

    1er juin, Anasazi Girl is back in the water.

    June, A family of four: James Burwick, Somira Sao, Tormentina (age 3) and Raivo (age 9 months). are attempting to set an unofficial crewed speed record - Portland, Maine (USA) to the Port of Caen, Normandy (FRANCE) - on a 40 foot monohull.

    17 juillet, arrivée à Cherbourg.

    25 aout, Lorient, en escale technique pour régler un probléme de mat:

    Prochaine escale Cape Town, puis Aukland.

    Décembre "Voiles et Voiliers",

  11. 2012

    12 mai, arrived in Fremantle, Western Australia.

    20 december, their third children, PEARL, was born in Auckland, New Zealand.

  12. 2013 Mise en vente

    Octobre, Anasazi Girl Open 40 Finot Conq - For sale - A Vendre, Description: Finot Conq designed, JMV built Open 40, Year: 2001, Price: US$ 249,000, Further Price Reduction, Lying: NZ

  13. 2014 Démâtage

    March, James Burwick: "During East-bound passage attempt from Auckland to Lorient, we dismasted our vessel 21 days out of Auckland, approximately 300 nm West of Diego Ramirez Islands."

  14. 2015 Démâtage

    Mai, James Burwick: "Anasazi Girl is rafted to a fleet of expedition sailing vessels at the Club Naval de Yates Micalvi. This is the second winter the boat will spend dismasted on the island. Our family will be the only full-time live-aboard crew at the Micalvi during the 2015 Austral winter season". http://anasaziracing.blogspot.fr/p/support-us

  15. 2016 Refit, modification

    December, new mast, and visit to Ushuaia

  16. 2017 Mise en vente

  19. 2021 Mise en vente

    September "Global Solo Challenge website", Around Alone Open 40 Anasazi Girl à vendre, The boat has been designed primarily for short-handed offshore competitions. It is an exceptional yacht, in terms of performance, quality and reliability. Therefore, every part of its building shall endeavor to save weight as much as possible – while keeping the costs at a reasonable level – yet retaining the necessary strength characteristics of a boat cruising far offshore with large loads due to sea or slamming, hence far within the requirements of Category A of the 94/25 EU RCD... ANASAZI GIRL. Carbon machine extremely capable for all oceans. Dimasted in Southern Ocean in March 2014. She sat in Chile until a new rig arrived Sept 20th 2016. She is up and ready. Shorter alloy rig. Engineered for Southern Ocean sailing. New main is Class 40 with 1 reef. Reason for sail. We no longer fit on board when in port. 2 adults and 4 children. 3 was ok but number 4 is squeezing us out.and now pregnant with number 5 Proven circumnavigator via the 3 great capes extremely strong and light she is very forgiving and when it get huge she will keep you alive.
