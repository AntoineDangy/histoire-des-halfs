  11. 2026 Mise en venteRefit, modification

    10 mars site Class 40, A Vendre MadMax is ready to compete for the 2026 Route du Rhum. Available from May or June. She is a David Raison design. Optimised and sailed with success by Axel Trehin, she ranked within the Top 5 of most of the races between 2021 and 2024.
