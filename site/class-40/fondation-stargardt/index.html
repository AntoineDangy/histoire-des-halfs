  4. 2023 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en venteRefit, modificationConstruction, mise à l’eau

    July Owen Clarke Design website, Class 40 - #180 For Sale - A Vendre, Built by Multiplast, ‘Lucente’ is the latest and most exciting VPLP Clak 40. She is the new generation scow bow launched in May 2022 which has been designed to perform fast and efficiently on all points of sail. In her first short season she proved her worth finishing 9 th in the Route du Rhum and 1st in the Lodi Group Trophy (40' Malouine). She has been prepared and optimised in all aspects with two full-time marine professionals since her launch. his high-performance vessel has been equipped and maintained to the highest standards with no expense spared. The Class 40 measurement states the RM = 318kg, by far the best numbers for any Clak 40. Polars and sail crossover chart made with Sailing Performance by Fabien Delahaye. She comes with a comprehensive and detailed inventory including new Axxon carbon rigging with 1 set of spreaders, new engine and new North Sails. All controls return to the highly protected cockpit which has a generous cuddy and coamings, allowing for sheltered trimming. This is a perfect opportunity to purchase an ‘as new’ Class 40 which is sold ready to race and free of any markings. LOA: 12.19 m Beam: 4.5 m Draft: 3.0 m Lying: Brittany, France Built: Multiplast Designer: VPLP Clak40 Launched: 2022 Price: €810,000 Ex VAT
