    For Sale - A Vendre, This particular Class 40 has done very little racing and comes fully equipped with a good inventory and in present ownership has had considerable investment with a recent refit and sensible upgrades all round to her equipment. All the hard work has been carried out and she is ready to sail away. Sensible offers may be considered on the asking price. Built 2007, Shipyard: Pogo Structures, Architects: Finot- Conq, Length: 12.18 m, Beam: 4.4 m, Keel draft: 3 m, Location: Spain
