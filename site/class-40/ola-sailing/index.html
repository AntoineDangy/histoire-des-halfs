  5. 2019 VictoireMise en vente

    3 may, La Duecento: 4e/75 ellapsed time Overall, Ivica KOSTELIC

    3 aout Class 40 website, For Sale Akilaria RC2 #122, fast, fun ans easy boat to sail in all condition....

    21 aout, Palermo-Montecarlo, 437 NM, elapsed time 100h34', 16e/36 ORC

    11 décembre, Class 40 website, Akilaria RC2 #122 for sale, "Ola" is a fast, fun and easy boat to sail in all conditions, proven winner in both inshore and offshore racing. Launched in 2012 and privately owned, she has been sailed mostly in the Adriatic and because of low mileage (less than 20 000 miles!) and careful upkeeping, it's condition is AS NEW. Boat is fully equipped and optimized for offshore shorthanded racing with two independent autopilots, watermaker, AIS, two VHF radios, Adrena navigation software and many more pros such as new running rigging, new forestay, new mainsail, carefully planned and adapted interior - all for a very reasonable price. She is ready to sail and keep on winning! Asking price 160000 € (VAT incl.) "Ola" is currently lying in Palermo, Italy but coming to Les Sables d'Olonne, France in September 2019, Inventory on request
