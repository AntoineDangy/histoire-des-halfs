  11. 2021 Mise en venteSponsorRefit, modification

    April, Owen Clarke Design Class 40 #90 For Sale, This Owen Clarke Design Class40 is in excellent structural conditions, sister-ship to #79 it was built to Category Zero standards to include additional transverse bulkheads. The boat has just been re commissioned after being out the water for 3 and a half years and needs some TLC to brink her to top form. This Owen Clarke Design Class40 is in excellent structural conditions, sister-ship to #79 it was built to Category Zero standards to include additional transverse bulkheads. The boat has just been re commissioned after being out the water for 3 and a half years and needs some TLC to brink her to top form. All graphics are vinyl wrap from previous sponsorship and can be removed. The mast is a Southern Spars mast brand new in 2015 with only about 2 years of actual use. All running rigging has been renewed in in 2021. The upwind sail inventory requires a new main and Solent: currently onboard are only a delivery mainsail and dacron staysail as well as 3 downwind sails in good order. Sail inventory and maintenance jobs still to be carried out will be reflected in the price to be negotiated with any interested party. Antifouled in 2021. Location:Currently in Santos Brazil with plans to ship the boat to Europe in May. Purchase in South America allows for a considerable discount. Length Overall: 12.192m Max Beam: 4.5m Draught: 3.00m Displacement: minimum 4500kg Build: Jaz Marine Cape Town

    Class: Class 40. Measured to class rules September 2009 (Updated 2015); Launched: September 2009; Construction: Epoxy glass composite with Corecell foam, from a female mould. Keel: Fabricated keel fin, Lead Bulb Cast from a female mould optimised weight in 2015
