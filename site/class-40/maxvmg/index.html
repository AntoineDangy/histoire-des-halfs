  10. 2018 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en vente

    7 juillet, Round the Island Race: 4e/7 Class 40 en 10h46' à 49'

    20 septembre Site Class 40, Our Akilaria RC1 Class 40 GBR 97 is currently seeking new owners. She is one of the latest of the first generation designs which have a fully useable interior for cruising and racing either solo or with up to 8 onboard. She has been operating for the last three years as a charter vessel, and since her launch in 2010 has only ever done European offshore races - not transatlantic and is in very good condition. She has a full North Sails wardrobe in good condition for age, well looked after, B&G H3000 with gyro pilot, MCA coded to operate commercially in the UK. A full inventory of equipment and sails is available on request. The commercial charter business model is sustainable, and we would like to offer shared or full ownership of the yacht with the current charter business based in the UK. The current skipper would be willing to continue to manage the commercial charters and maintenance of the boat. Alternatively she will be reluctantly for sale for £120,000 GBP including VAT. Fortissimo can be viewed in Gosport, UK.

  12. 2021 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en vente

    April "Global Solo Challenge website" , Class40 Akilaria RC1 (Mark Lombard) – n.97 – For Sale, A very impressive Akilaria RC1 with racing cockpit and central navigation station, built in 2008 but only launched in July 2010 (Class 40 no. 97) and little used by her one original owner. This was one of the last of the RC1s on the water with all the very latest features of that generation built into her. Major upgrades are replaced rod rigging in 2015, new check stays in 2016, new PBO inner forestay in 2017, New wooden tillers in 2019, New A2 spinnaker from GP Sails (insurance replacement) Old North A2 was split in two but has now been repaired and is a good sail. The new owners have added an Origo alcohol oven and stove – the alcohol ovens are rare and expensive. Also added, a battery charger, shore power, calorifier for hot water, electric pumped water and a fridge. All easy to remove for racing, but makes cruising a very enjoyable experience, while sacrificing little in terms of performance. This is a genuine dual-purpose Class 40 that can be cruised in comfort but still surfs and hits Class 40 speeds. Perfect for channel hops, offshore racing, fast passage long distance cruising to remote locations or to access your favourite bucket list regattas. These earlier generation Class 40s tick all the boxes and are now becoming extremely popular as rapid cruisers. The embellishments to Fortissimo make for an easy transition from racer to cruiser or vice versa and she really is ready to sail away. LOA: 12.18m Beam: 4.49m Draft: 3.0m, Builder: MC TEC, Lying: South Coast UK
