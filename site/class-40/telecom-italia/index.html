  21. 2021 Refit, modification

    Février, toujours à Lorient, vu par Stéphane

    26 avril site Class 40, Plan Verdier 55 Tyker evolution à louer, Location pour Transat Jacques Vabre 2021, bateau encore performant, nombreuses modifications, mât 2017 Aaxon, processeur HR NKE, panneaux solaire,

    28 September Class 40 website, To rent per year, per event/race or for coaching lessons, The boat is a prototype designed by G.Verdier, she has won many races, solid, fast and she had 2 major refit in 2017/18 then in 2019/20. The ballast are more at the back of the boat and she has a new mast Aaxon late 2017. Main sail/J1/J2/A2 new from 2019/20 by North sails, Code0/5 and medium A6 from 2017/18 by north sails, New Garmin instruments/plotter, HR NKE processor, 2 autopilots + 2 BUS calculators, Navigation laptop time zero, 11 solar panels, many modifications to be Cat0 like watertight door bulkhead installed.

  23. 2023 VictoireVendu, nouveau propriétaireRefit, modification

    11 January site Class 40, Tyker 40 Evo 2 ‘Magale’ Class 40 #55, Originally campaigned by Giovanni Soldini under the colours of ‘Telecom Italia’ and with many wins to her name to include TJV 2007, Artemis Transat 2008 and 8 podium places 2009/10 she has an impressive race record. She is a fast prototype boat that has evolved within the racing rules, with many modifications and optimisations since launch. With a new Axxon carbon mast & standing rigging in 2018 and a suite of new North Sails (2020), barely used, she is a seriously capable boat.
