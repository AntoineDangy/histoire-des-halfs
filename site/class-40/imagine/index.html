    1 September, Class 40 #157 For Sale - A Vendre, Launched in 2019 by Cape Racing Yachts. ‘Imagine’ was a 3rd place finisher in the 2019 Normandy Channel Race in the competent hands of Jörg Riechers. This boat has seen some post launch optimisation, re-faired appendages. Professionally maintained, clean, tidy, low mileage example of a fifth generation Owen Clarke Design Class 40. The design is polyvalent in concept meaning that it has been set up to be an all-rounder and without the constraints of being optimised for a specific race or series of races. There is scope to reconfigure the boat for events where a more reaching orientated route might be of benefit.
