    Décember, Boat name "Saltystreaker" Eygthene24 for sale. Excellent condition throughout. Ron Holland Design built in 1982. Mast head rig with big sail inventory 2 mains, 2 jibs, Storm Jib, 2 Spinnakers, New Plastimo Furling Gear 2009. Garmin GPS Map 450 2008. ICOM IC-M401E VHF 2008. Purpose Built Trailer 2008 with towing lights. Depth Sounder Target 2 NASA Marine 2008. Dehumidifier 2008. Internal woodwork upgraded 2008. Porta Loo 2008. Plough anchor with chain and rope. Normal fenders and mooring lines. Gimbled gas ring cooker 2008. Solar Panel for battery charging 2008. Engine 9.9HP Johnson 2 stroke new 2008. As can be seen from the above description a lot of new equipment has been fitted to Saltystreaker, she is presented in a ready to go condition with no additional work required. Status: Price Reduced, Price: EUR 9,000, Location: Co Cork Ireland
