CT-25 or Polaris or Baron 76, plan Larionov,

Designed by Larionov 84 with, in Russia, hull dated between 1984-1991.

Built in Estonia first at a plant called VERF (Tallinn Sporting Ship factory), after liberation NAVICO.

Estonians call the boat type "Polaris" named after the prototype. Some attempts where made early 90' to sell west-ward under the names Polaris, Z80 and Race77.

Longueur: 7,60m, largeur: 2,7m,

Baron 76, Test from magazine "Yacht" 22/91
