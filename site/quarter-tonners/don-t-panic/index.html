  16. 2018 VictoireMise en venteRefit, modification

    August, For Sale 'Panic' (GBR 7299), 2014 Concours D’Elegance winner, GBR 7299. IRC2015 0.910. 1984 FULLY IRC Optimised,Peter Gimpel Designed Quarter Ton Racing Boat. A complete photographic history here: https://www.flickr.com/photos/geoffbsbs/sets/72157627684730108 Now undercover in Brightlingsea Essex, where she is available for viewing. During the winter 2011-2012 ALL deck fittings were replaced with Harken and in all cases soft attached were used. The boat was refaired and sprayed with Awlgrip in 2014. During the winter of 2014/15 the following was done. The cockpit floor was replaced. Central control pod to control the kicker.., traveller and back stay. The whole interior was sand blasted and then painted in white Durepox, with an additional coat of Bilgex in the bilge. New rudder bearing. New Stainless Steel tiller. Selden keel stepped Mast and Selden Boom 2013. 1 Carbon Fibre Spinnaker pole 2013; 1 Carbon Fibre Spinnaker pole 2011 Spare. Sails (All by Advantage Sails) A brand new main and 2 Jibs (1XNo1 and 1XNo1 Light) Only used 2015 QTC and 2015 Cowes Week. Mainsail 2012, Lightweight No1 Jib 2013. Heavy No1 Jib 2012, Heavy No2 Jib 2012, Spinnakers: .6 oz 2011 Red and White 2011 .75oz 2011 Green and Purple 2011 .75 oz 2012 Black with TB4E logo. .75 oz 2014 Black with Sail4Cancer logo last used QTC2014/15 .5 oz 2014 used QTC 2014/15; Electrical: Full Tack Tick Package 2011, Velocitek with spare batteries and charger, Trailer new 2012.
