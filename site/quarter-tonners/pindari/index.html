I brought it from a 82 year old who'd used to race its been in a barn for 10 years, the price does not include the outboard i payed 3000 pounds for this it has only done 7 hrs from new would include for further 1800 pounds or sell separate to be honest the engine is a bit big for racing ( to heavy ) down below there is a couple of bunks not a lot of room stove etc and electrics all new lights and Arial fitted just needs connecting for night time, location: Devon UK
