Chronologie détaillée

  1. 1967 Construction, mise à l’eau

    plan MAURIC & Gaubert, built in Marseille, Challenger, L 7,2 m, B 2,3 m, T 1,4 m

  2. 1967 Victoire

    A Kiel.

    "Here the boat in the original setup.

    'Piranha II' (Challenger 1/4 tonner), winner of the 1968 QTC in Breskens with skipper Hans Kortekaas. For the 1968 QTC the boat was modified. The stern was cut off in a V shape and a frame was placed for the backstay."

  3. 1968 Victoire

    Modified to fit the Quarter Ton Cup 1968 rules, equipped with a bulbous keel and hollow stern.

    QUARTER TON CUP, Breskens, Pays Bas: 1er/12, Hans KORTEKAAS

    "Yachting World Annual 1970",

  4. 2005
    Devient Krygertje

    et

    "Named Krygertje when we found her. Restored in 2005, re-christening her Piranha II.

    Since then, cruised with great pleasure." http://www.spiras.de

  5. 2005
    Devient Pirhana II

    "After my overhaul."

  6. 2010 Mise en venteRefit, modification

    Février: A Vendre 1968, Challenger Piranha II im quarter-ton trim, L 7,2 m, B 2,3 m, T 1,4 m, AB Johnson 10 PS e-start. Komplett restauriert 2004 /2005. Topgetakelt, 6 Vorsegel, 1 Großsegel, Spinnaker, diverse Trimmöglichkeiten incl Backstagen . Baumpersenning, Achterdeckpersenning, Autopilot, GPS, VDO Sumlog, VDO Echolot, VDO Kompass, VDO Windmessanlage, Seefunk, BSH Beleuchtung. 4 Kojen, Polster neu bezogen (2005). Bug- und Heckkorb vorhanden. Liegeplatz Berlin VB 4.800 € Tel: 09131-5332128

    Photos: http://www.spiras.de

  7. 201? Vendu, nouveau propriétaire

    Sold to a Berlineer.
