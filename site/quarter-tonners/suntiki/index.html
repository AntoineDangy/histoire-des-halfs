Chronologie détaillée

  1. 1976 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireConstruction, mise à l’eau

    plan VAN DE STADT, Oceaan 25 built at Poly Boats in Ossendrecht

    Comment from Facebook 2020: "The hull was built in 1976 at Poly Boats in Ossendrecht under guidance of the designer Cees van Tongeren (Van de Stadt Design). Theun Hoogmoed finished the boat in 1977 in Zaandam. The boat was around 1,500 kg of which 750kg in the keel. Her more or less sisterships were 'OCEAAN 4' (that finished 6th at the 1977 Quarter Ton Cup in Helsinki) and 'OCEAAN 3'. Fred Knitel sailed a lot of races, but sold her when he was starting a family."

    Information and picture from Dutch QT Facebook: "The hull was built in 1976 at Poly Boats in Ossendrecht under guidance of the designer Cees van Tongeren (Van de Stadt Design). Theun Hoogmoed finished the boat in 1977 in Zaandam. The boat was around 1.500kg of which 750kg in the keel. Her more or less sisterships were 'OCEAAN 4' (that finished 6th at the 1977 Quarter Ton Cup in Helsinki) and 'OCEAAN 3'. Fred Knitel sailed a lot of races, but sold her when he was starting a family. 'Suntiki' is now for sale in Sneek"
