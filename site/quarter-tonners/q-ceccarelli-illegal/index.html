Chronologie détaillée

  1. 1990 Construction, mise à l’eau

    plan Ceccarelli, Design Number: CYD 0190 (C), built by Cantiere Nautico Italo Galetti- Peschiera del Garda – Italia, carbon

    LOA mt.8.50, LWL mt.7.45, BMAX mt.2.65, DRAFT mt.1.90, DSPL kg. 1350

    Alkis LOGOTHETIS comments, in 2010: "Pandemonion's construction began (confirmed by the competent authorities) end of November - first days of December 1989 and was measured in Lago di Garda beginning June 1990 to participate in the 1/4 Ton Cup in Bayona (Spain) in September 1990. The rules allowance was that a carbon boat was acceptable provided the construction had begun prior to December 31st 1989 and completion and measurement prior to June 30th 1990!

    So Pandemonion was the last boat built in carbon. This was made possible by the fact that the mould existed because 2 boats [Marfrio, Audace II] had been built already.

    The mould was modified somewhat at the aft section, but the big difference was made by the keel, which was our contribution to the design and the small rudder blade, which in the right conditions and the old sail plan was outstanding."
