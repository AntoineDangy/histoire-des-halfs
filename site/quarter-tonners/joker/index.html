  2. 1979 Construction, mise à l’eau

    QUARTER TON CUP, San Remo: 32 - 12 - 34 - 19 - 33 = ?e/56. Fred IMHOFF

    Comment and Pictures from Dutch QTC Facebook 2019, "The Jezequel 1/4 tonner 'Joker' arriving in The Netherlands from Italy where she was built. Owner Fred Imhoff sailed the 1979 Quarter Ton Worlds in San Remo, and lots of regattas in the Netherlands with the boat. The boat also sailed the 1984 Quarter Ton Worlds in Nieuwpoort. Thanks for the pictures Cornelis Baas!"

  3. 1984 Abandon de courseGrande course

    1984 26 August, QUARTER TON CUP, Nieuwpoort: 19 - 17 - dnf - dnc = 24e/26. C.J.A. HORST

    The ‘Joker’ is designed and built in 1979 by Alain Jézéquel for Fred Imhoff. Imhoff has sailed her very successful until the end of the IOR quartton rule. The ‘Joker’ is a boat with good looks and a rich history.

  5. 2010 Mise en vente

    Current owner owns the boat since 2000 and restored the boat completely. The owner is industrial design engineer and combines a passion for sailing with a passion for overhaul and maintenance. The boat is in racing condition and easy to maintain. The Joker got a new paintjob in October 2009 and the hull is in a 100% condition. Under the water line the hull is spray painted with Coppercoat: no maintenance or anti-fouling needed for the next 10 years. The beautiful Sparcraft F78 mast is supplied by Neutmast in 2006. In recent years the boat has been used for racing and cruising to the UK. Owner is aiming for short handed offshore racing. This boat is too small for that purpose, that is why she is for sale. All tests are allowed. For more information see details below. Construction: Hull and Deck: full kevlar-epoxy inner scale, foam core (12 mm) and glass-epoxy outer scale (approx 1,8 mm) Mainframe: Stainless steel frame laminated in the hull. All main forces are lead to this frame: rigging, keel, engine and mast are mounted onto this frame. Keel: fin keel of casted lead with a steel inner frame. Rudder: Balanced rudder (2006). Shaft made of aluminum 6000 alloy with plywood rudder blade and finished with glass-epoxy. Shaft runs in 3 nylon bearings in titanium tube. Certificates: ORC certificate present, NED2736. Asking 28,000 Euros.

    Source: Jac Sandberg
