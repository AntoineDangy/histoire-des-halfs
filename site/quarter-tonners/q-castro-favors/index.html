  9. 2012 Mise en vente
    Devient Hendricks

    For Sale Hendricks - Castro MG26 (1985) IRL 1000, Currently asking £10,000, IRC 0.907, Beam: 3.00m Draft: 1.59m, LOA: 7.65m

    Fibreglass balsa core construction. Epoxy painted and faired. Proctor aluminium mast with runners, jumpers and checkstays. Harken Lewmar and Spinlock. Extensive racing and cruising sail wardrobe including: 2 mains (One 3D moulded with very light use) 4 spinnakers, 5 racing North head sails and storm sails, Well maintained and serviced Beta 13.5hp (2000) , Raymarine instruments: ST60 Wind, Depth and Speed with displays on deck. DSC VHF, S1000 Tiller pilot and remote control, Powered by two engine charged 75 Amp batteries with isolators. Electric and manual bilge pumps. Hendrick's has had alot of time and money invested on her and with all gear onboard of a very high specification she is ready for a competitive 2012 campaign. Currently located on a well maintained road legal trailer (included) in Lossiemouth (IV316NT).
