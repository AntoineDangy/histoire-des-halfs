  10. 2007 VictoireVendu, nouveau propriétaire

    Info and picture from Site Quarter GB: "Ed then designed the Starflash of which there are 2 or 3 joining the fleet, and the development of that was Enigma. Designed for a Northern Irish based yachtsman and built in his garage, Enigma was an extremely successful boat being particularly fast in light airs. Morty found Enigma lying in the corner of a yard in Plymouth, tracked the owner down, and bought it back to Hamble. As Ed told the story at the QTC dinner in 2006, he was phoned up and told that he had bought Enigma back. Anthony Haines restored the boat during the winter of 2005/6, launched it the day before the QTC and Ed subsequently was the worthy winner of the QTC in 2006."

    23 juin: Tour de l'ile de Wight: inscrit cat IRC 3C, départ à 06h00.Coeff: 0,905. Michael BENAKIS
