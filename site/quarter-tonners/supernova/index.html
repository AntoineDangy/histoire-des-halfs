  10. 2014 VictoireMise en vente

    For Sale, Description: Looking for a great performer for club racing, or to join the growing Quarter Ton Fleet in time for the European Championships in Ireland 2013 ? Professionally Re-fit in 2007 to win the Conquors D’Elegance at the Quarter Ton Cup in Cowes. Supernova performs at the very top of Class 3 with many victories on Ireland’s East Coast including Winner of IRC ‘Boat of the Week’ title in Volvo Dun Laoghaire Week 2011, Lambay Trophy Winner 2008 / 2009 / 2011, East Coast Champion 2010, Dublin Bay Cruiser 3 Champion 2009-2012; 35th in Round the Island Race IOW (1700 boats), Fully optimized with ultra competitive .871 Endorsed Irc Cert, All Harken / Spinlock fittings and top specification running rigging. 2 full suits of sails. Tactick wireless speedo - 3.5 hp Mariner rarely used. Dry Sailed the last 5 years (centre lifting point) and wintered indoors. Fine yard trailer but some work required to make her road legal.

    March, received this picture from Vincent Delany, ashore at Royal Irish Yacht Club
