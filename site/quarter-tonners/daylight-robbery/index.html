  6. 2018

    Picture from Dutch QT Facebook,

    Andrew Ketteringham: "Thank you for posting up the pictures of Daylight Robbery, I have owned her for the last couple of years after purchasing of eBay. I spent the summer of 2016 restoring to a reasonable level", "I am keen to learn the History of Daylight Robbery l know she was commissioned by Val Moody Guernsey Yacht Club and built by the Jardine bros, Val raced her for several years competing in many of the regattas and cross channel races. I have uploaded some pictures of how she looks now. Plans for 2018 season replace mast head rig with a fractional rig non overlapping jibs and a larger main, .https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qi8HA3_M96s"

    "Daylight Robbery in its 2017 colour scheme. Lot of work carried out over the past 12 months and looking rather different now but a lot more work still to complete new bigger rig swappping from masthead to fractional also new non overlapping jib"
