  7. 2016 AvarieVendu, nouveau propriétaireRefit, modification

    Upgraded, new IRC optimised keel, new colors, new cockpit...

    29 February, from British QT Class: "Protis, Bruno Trouble's 1981 Fauroux design which won the QTC in Marseille that year, was found by Morty in Italy and has been bought by Diarmid de Burgh-Milne. She is

    currently undergoing a full refit at Casse Tete Marine ready for the 2016 season."
