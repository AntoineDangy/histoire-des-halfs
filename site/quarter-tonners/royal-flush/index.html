  13. 2020 Mise en venteRefit, modification

    November, For sale Royal Flush 1985 1/4 ton world Champ refitted, Rand 230,000, Location: Gordon's Bay, Helderberg, South Africa, Description: The boat was completely restored with modern light weight material. Spared no expense when it came to the refit. PVC 80 foam deck with eglass and carbon fiber reinforcement. There are currently two world Cup regattas for QT and the rule is you are not allowed to build a new one, you have to find and restore one. If you want to go race against all the best sailer in the world for a fraction of the price this is the boat for you. New corbonfiber bow sprit New corbonfiber rudder New carbonfiber tiller. New sails, Ulman Fiberparth genoa, North sails dacron jib, North sails dacron main sail, two spinnakers and a blooper. 6hp suzuki 4 stroke outboard. She has been racing in Gordon's Bay for the last two years and have won just about every race.
