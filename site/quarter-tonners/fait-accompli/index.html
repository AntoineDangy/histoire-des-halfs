  6. 2024 Mise en vente

    Januray, 1978 Starflash Quarter Tonner ‘Fait Accompli’ for sale. One of twelve Starflash’s built - designed by Ed Dubois. Superb racing record in Belfast, Strangford & Lough Neagh. A great boat to race in the Quarter Tonner fleet. Current owner has well looked after the boat for the last 9 years with plenty of upgrades for racing: - Double tuff luff (new in 2015). - Selden deck organisers. - Spinlock clutches (new in 2019). - Aluminium Spinnaker Pole (new in 2017). - All halyards, control lines, sheets replaced in 2017. - All standing rigging replaced in 2017. - Selden Mainsheet track (new in 2017). - Adjustsble Barton Genoa tracks (new in 2017). - Spinlock tiller extension.Instruments: - Two compasses. - NASA Speed & Depth Sounder. - VHF Radio. Sails: - 2014 Hyde Mainsail Dacron: Fair condition. - 2014 Hyde No1 Genoa Kevlar: Fair condition. - 2014 Hyde Spinnaker 29sqm: Fair condition. - 2017 Hyde Spinnaker 36sqm: Good condition. - 2019 Hyde Mainsail Carbon: Very good condition (only used for regattas). - 2019 Hyde No1 Genoa Carbon: Very good condition (only used for regattas). - 2019 Hyde No3 Jib Dacron: Good condition. Internal: Boat has been stripped out for racing but there is 4 single berths with cushions & internal sink. Boat is currently located at Antrim Boat Club, Lough Neagh.
