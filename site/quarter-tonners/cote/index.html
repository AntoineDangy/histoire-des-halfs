  12. 2016 Mise en vente

    2016 9 mars, A Vendre - For sale, lying Solent, UK, this relatively radical design was built for the Crown Prince Felipe of Spain to campaign in the 1990 worlds in Bayona in wich she finished runner up. We found the boat (courtesy of Morty) in a very sorry state sat outside in Vigo late 2008. Following a full restoration project amounting to something like 1000 hours work throughout 2009 she made it to the start line of QTC and became the 2010 QTC Champion. £40,000. (quartertonclass website)
