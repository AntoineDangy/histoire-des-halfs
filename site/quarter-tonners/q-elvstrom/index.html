Elvstrom KT ou 1/4 Ton,

A flush deck racer intended for competition under the IOR 1/4 ton rule.

#1 'Aphrodite', #2 'Go', 'Another'Go',.

Became prototype for BIANCA 26, BIANCA 28.

A later version was built in Canada called the WHITBY 27.

Hull Type: Fin with rudder on skeg

Rigging Type: Masthead Sloop
