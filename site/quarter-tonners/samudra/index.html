  3. 2012 Mise en vente

    For Sale, Classic Sparkman and Stephens Quarter Ton cruiser/racer, built by W A Souter and Sons (Cowes) in 1973. British Registered No 360442, Dimensions: LOA 24 ft, LWL 21 ft, Beam 9 ft, Draft 4 ft 10 ins. Registered tonnage 4.41, Construction: Finn/Skeg Mahogany/teak cold moulded. Tiller steering, External cast lead fin keel. Price £7,900 includes purpose built twin axle road trailer with 4 new tyres. Engine Volvo MD5A diesel engine. 9.5 hp inboard. 1 fuel tank - 2 gallon capacity 1 folding prop; Sails North, Hoods, Banks 10 - all good condition, Harken furling headsail - 1993; Mainsail - 2 full length battens 2000 - hardly used; 135% genoa 2000 hardly used... 2 x Lewmar self tailing winches, 2 Lewmar mast winches, All halyards lead back to the cockpit via snap blocks, Accommodation: 4 berths in 2 cabins, Samudra was built to exceptionally high standard, impossibly expensive to reproduce today. A joy to windward and wonderfully dry because of the fine entry and pronounced tumblehome she has good directional stability downwind. A safe dry seaboat.
