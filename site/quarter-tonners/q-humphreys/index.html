    For Sale, IRL 508 Full Circle (formerly Quest) 1987 Rob Humphreys 1/4 tonner Offers around £17895, A well regarded 1/4. Currently not racing often enough to fulfill her potential (hence sale). Sails all Norths; The boat wasn't sailed in '08 and has only managed to make a few events each year since so the sails are in good condition. Mariner 4hp 2 stroke outboard, Fulll Circle is currently ashore on her road trailer in Hamble. Contact Robin Russell
