  1. 1987 Construction, mise à l’eau

    plan Tony Castro, MG26 modified

    Seahorse QTC 87: "McDonald's is a MG 26, that is she is made out of the same moulds and thu has the same hull and deck shape as the standard boat. Built by Stephen Etheridge, her construction is totally different though as she is of the 'exotic family'. The result is of course less weight particularly in the ends and a boat that is much easier to get into rating trim. Her rated displacement is nearly 300kgs less for the same sail area as the standard boats and the corresponding ratios stand out in the MG fleet."

  13. 2009 VictoireVendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en venteRefit, modification

    "The original McDonalds is still racing in Sweden"

    Mai: Macdonald is for sale, MG 26 1/4 tonner, The former 1/4 tonner Macdonald is for sale, winner of 1/4 tonner cup 1987-1988. Asking price is 10-12.000 €, Built 1985 in Southampton and design by Tony Castro. The boat is built in carbon fiber. The boat is located in Malmo Sweden. 7,60 * 2.95 mtr displacement: 1.140 Kg Hull carbon fiber and Kevlar. The boat was refitted and rebuild 1995 with a new cockpit and new keel.

    Acheté par Pierre PARIS
