  6. 2010 Victoire

    The Tequila Sport 24 Quarter Tonner was built in 1973 for the Quarter Ton Cup. It benefits from having arrived before the extremes of the rule, and is therefore easy to sail short handed. Most recent wins have been with a crew of 2. It has also had much recent work carried out. The Tequila is ready to go for some good, none expensive club racing. Survey November 2009 available. These boat details are subject to contract. Note: Offers on the asking price may be considered. This Tequila Sport 24 Quarter Tonner has been personally photographed and viewed by Andrew Topham of Boatshed North Wales. LOA 7.2m (23'8"), LWL 5.2m (17'0"), Beam 2.4m (8'0"), Draft Max 1.3m (4'1"), Displacement 1,410kg (3,108.0lbs), Headroom 1.5m (4'11"), Storage On trailer, Tequila Sport 24 Quarter Tonner Price £4,995.00

    May, Professionally modified & maintained Tequila Sport 24 - Olivier Gibert designed for 1/4 ton cup in 1973 and it won New Bruce Farr balanced spade rudder, skeg removed and cockpit / lazerette removed and replaced with open plan racing cockpit All new top of the range Harken Deck Gear and new running rigging. New suit of sails used maybe 20 times including Main, Jib, 3 kites (1 symmetric and 1 asymmetric) and a code zero used literally once! Full set of tacktick instruments. Carbonautica carbon tiller. Professionally resprayed to mirror finish in Awlcraft 2000 "Whisper Grey". Lightweight Teak & Holly floorboards. Interior just repainted with flowcoat and decks just repainted with non slip deck paint. New Z-Spars spinnaker pole and boom. Includes 4 wheel braked trailor with moulded cushioned support saddles with new alloy wheels and complete new front end and new brake pads all round.
