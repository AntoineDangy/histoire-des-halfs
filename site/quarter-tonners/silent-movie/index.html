    Baptisé Silent Movie GBR 7235

    Navigue sur la cote Ouest Grande Bretagne. ?

    Info and picture from Richard Salter: "Sorry this is not the best picture quality, but shows QT Silent Movie in her previous life (maybe 1987, at Dartmouth Regatta, then owned (I think) by Peter Rutter. The boat then passed to Paul Martin as part of an exchange for HT K5494 Lepus Lazuli, which became the first Quokka. Movie subsequently reverted to her original name Espada. I sailed on both boats during the 80’s."
