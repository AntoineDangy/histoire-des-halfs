  43. 2012 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en vente

    Salaminor (GBR 7292) is a 1982 Bolero, up for sale due to new family commitments. She has been raced for several years on the Clyde. She has been well maintained - front bulkhead. and well completely replaced two years ago. Due to a new family she has been out of the water since August 2010 and is now up for sale. We have had an exceptional amount of fun racing this boat and would like to see her continue her success with a new owner. The boat has been getting dried out while out of the water is planned to get a new application of Gelshield in Spring 2012 - hence the lower asking price. Accommodation: 4 berths in two abins (2 large quarter berhts) although both very basic as the boat has been used primarily for racing as opposed to any cruising. Rigging - 7 or 8 years old with in line spreaders and running backstays. Excellent condition. Sails: Main (kevlar - 7 years), No 1 Genoa (Kevlar 6 years), No 2 Genoa (Dacron - 3 years), No 3 Genoa (battened, 6 years, but as good as new - brilliant sail), No 1 spi (Black - 2 years), No 2 spinnaker (multicoloured - getting on a bit but good in a breeze), Instruments: GPS, Log, Windspeed and direction, compass and depth. 3.3 HP Mercury - well maintained from new and has never let us down. Extra - comes with tender, and an option on a yard trailer (not for public road use), Price £6,000, lying Clyde. Contact Ken Brown on 0783 441 6710

    Picture from Quarter Ton Class.
