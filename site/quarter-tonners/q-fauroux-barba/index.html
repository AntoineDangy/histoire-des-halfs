  10. 2019 Vendu, nouveau propriétaire

    From Dutch QT Class facebook, George Nottis: "We sailed Outrage GR-49323, a Faroux design. A few years ago she was sailed as H-76. We modified all deck layout and control systems and reduced her winches from 6 to 2. She was quite old to be competitive with the new then Ceccarelli Design and the Vismaras, but we extracted every bit she had to give. I believe Dionisis Kourtesis is related to the boat now. I think he bought it some years after that. In the meantime she raced in club races around Thessaloniki. I will check for photos."
