    For Sale, DIAMOND GBR 6498 One off Quarter Tonner, Built by West Marine in 1984 to a Dubois design. Continuously updated , improved and maintained. Hull faired by Ovington Boats in 2006 and dry sailed since. New stainless steel pulpit, pushpit, and twin guardwire stanchions 2006. Rig redesigned by Dubois office in 2009 and structural work carried out by Ian Lovering of Ashdown Marine . New single spreader Z Spar rig in 2010. Further rig improvements in 2011 – halyards, traveller, backstay controls led beneath deck to central pod in cockpit. Full set North 3DL sails new in 2011. Plus older 3DL sails. Carbon spinnaker pole. New Mills designed lead keel fitted 2011. Deck gear mainly Harken. Lewmar winches. PRICE £28,000, Contact Graydon Dawson
