  10. 2016 VictoireMise en venteRefit, modification

    Juillet, "Sinergy" A Vendre - For sale, Dufour 1300, Sydney, price : $14,990.00, Brand : Dufour Length : 24 Feet, Year : 1975 Hull Style : Single Hull ,Type : Fibreglass, Power Type : Sail Engine Type : Outboard Condition : Used State, New South Wales, PRICE NEGOATIABLE! A genuine 2 owner vessel with original interior - was the 1975 Sydney Boat Show Boat of the Year and purchased at the show by the first owner. She was sailed extensively in harbour racing up until 2000 from the RSYS and then moored at Neutral Bay until purchased in 2007 by the current owner . Major refit completed in 2009 included all standing / running rigging, rewire mast, new Nav lights, re-gel coat deck and repaint hull. Main sheet winches were removed and refurbished including re-chroming the bronze winch drums. Sails are in near-new condition. This yacht is a great little club racer with potential to win and also a sound and seaworthy cruiser for short coastal trips and overnight stays around Sydney Harbour. With virtually nothing to spend, this boat is a perfect, low cost opportunity for someone keen to enjoy sailing and racing.
