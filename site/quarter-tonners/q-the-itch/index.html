Chronologie détaillée

  1. 1979 Construction, mise à l’eau

    plan Monhaupt-Rode, builder: Broager G. Denmark

  2. 1979 Vendu, nouveau propriétaire

    Quarter Ton Cup, San Remo: 6 - 36 - 43 - 14 - dns = ??e/56. Roy CUNDIF, US owner

    Sold to German Owner

    Information and picture from Dutch QT Class Facebook, November 2015: "Roy was so impressed with the Mohnhaupt 1/4 tonner '4 Samurais' that Mohnhaupt was asked to design a new boat for the 1979 Worlds in San Remo. 'Parade' (US-25) was a fine boat and very fast downwind. A protest in one race and poor navigation put the team out of the running...'Parade' was sold to Germany after the 1979 Worlds., and in 2000 she moved to the Netherlands. As 'The It...ch' she raced a lot of regatta's. Hope to see her at the 2016 DQTC!"

  3. 1979
    Baptisé Punzig Turteldrom GER 1925

    with IMS certificate, G & R Werner

  4. 2000 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireRefit, modification

    New owner: Berry AARTS and refitted

    Won a few regatta's in IMS races. two times overall yearprices
