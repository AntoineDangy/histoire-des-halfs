Chronologie détaillée

  1. 1992 Construction, mise à l’eau

    plan CECCARELLI, Builder: Galetti, 7,65 x 2,617m x 1316kg, (BA:2020, GD:683).

  2. 1992 Grande course

    QUARTER TON CUP, Chioggi: 11 - 3 - 13 - 17 - 21 =80.25pts, 14e/27. BUZZI

  3. 1993 Abandon de courseGrande course

    1993 Italian Champ (Marciana Marina, Elba Island): dnf - 6 - 5 - 5 - 8 = 6e/12, rating 18.55', Massimo BUZZI

    7 septembre, QUARTER TON CUP, Bayona: 16 - 18 - 14 - 11 - 11 - 13 - ? = 15e/21

  4. 1994
    Devient Agricole ITA 12294

    Certificat IOR

  5. 1997 VictoireGrande course
    Devient Isola D'Elba ITA 12294

    15 septembre, World Championship ILC 25 and Quarter Ton Cup, Italie: 3 - 7 - 3 - 9 - 7 = 6e/13 et 1er/5 Quarters, Stephan CHIAPPI

  6. 2006
    Devient Carlos V ITA 12294

    31 octobre, Trofeo Challenge "La Nuova", Sardaigne:1 - 6 - 2 = 2e/5 classe F. Giorgio MAGGIONI, Y. C. Calghero

  7. 2009
    Devient Giancagi’ ITA 12294

    18 octobre-15 novembre, Trofeo Invernale A Squadre, Alghero, NW Sardaigne: 10 - 2 - 2 - 6 - 5 = 5e/9, Giorgio MAGGIONI

  8. 2012 Vendu, nouveau propriétaire

    September, Boat sold to Declan Byrne and Richard Clery in Alghero, Sardinia, then trailer to Dublin via Marseille and Cherbourg.

  9. 2014 Refit, modification

    June, pictures received from Declan Byrne, co-owner, the boat is based in Howth Yacht Club, Dublin, Ireland and under refit.
