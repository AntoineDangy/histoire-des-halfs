  10. 2014 Mise en vente

    From Dutch QT Facebook:

    "Last weekend we sailed the Lenco regatta / DQTC 2014 # 2. On the parking lot outside the marina I found a Nissen 1/4 tonner called 'Parbleu'. In the back of the boat '1984 IOR design NISSEN' is carved out. The boat was built at the Yacht- ...und Bootswerft of Günter Knierim in Laboe, but I think most Nissen boats were built over there.Three Nissen boats sailed the 1984 QTC in Nieuwpoort. These boats were 'Schowi' (G 3274), 'Attacke' (G 3372) and 'Randale' (G 2004). 'Showi' is called 'Brown Sugar' at the moment and is based near Hamburg on the River Elbe. 'Randale' was for sale in the Netherlands some time ago, so 'Parbleu' is probably the old 'Attacke'. Does anyone have more info? 'Attacke' finished 2nd at the 1986 QTC in Rungsted, Danmark"

  16. 2019 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en vente

    March, from Dutch QT Class Facebook: "December 2018 a beautiful wooden Nissen 1/4 tonner was for sale in Austria. The ad is no longer available so the boat is probaby sold. Where is she now? Anyone more info?

    She was built at Knierim Yachtbau in Kiel (Germany). Builder Günter Knierim and designer Georg Nissen finished 2nd at the 1986 Quarter Ton Cup in Denmark as 'Attacke' (G-597) and Thomas Jungblut finished 3rd at the 1988 Quarter Ton Cup in Germany as 'Polyant' (G-597)."
