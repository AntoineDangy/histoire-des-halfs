  13. 2013 VictoireMise en venteSponsor
    Devient Edge of Reason

    Mai, A Vendre, Chauss Europ Trimaran € 230.000, The Edge of Reason is as pedigreed a multihull as you can get. This is an Orma 60 trimaran, which at one time not long ago, was the fastest sailing class in the world. The boat was designed by the legendary Nigel Irens, and built by the equally legendary Multiplast yard in France. If you have ever wanted a boat that is beyond anything most of us can imagine, this is it. This boat is capable of 400-500 mile days. Think about that for a while! Originally built 1994 as "Haute Normandie", sponsor change 96 as "Chauss Europ", 98/9 "Groupe Andre". Built for and campaigned by Paul Vatine. winner of several transatlantic and offshore races. An early generation Orma 60 before they started to pare the weights to the point that they became too fragile

  17. 2025 Vendu, nouveau propriétaire

    Mars, Panama, reçu de Yannick,

    Comment From Facebook 2025 "Originally Paul Vatine's ORMA60 this was refurbished, lengthened and converted into cruiser/racer by Multiplast around 10yrs ago. It had a new 80ft carbon rig and sat in storage for several years in Panama before it was purchased by a cruiser sailor with the goal to make it a full-time livaboard. After reinstalling the rig and hardware it was demasted while enroute to Puerto Rico and was towed back to where it has started out a few days earlier and then sold. The new owner is planning to convert it to a mv charterboat and not planning to replace the rig....."
