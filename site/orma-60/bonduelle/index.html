  10. 2012 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireRefit, modification

    15 mars, Artemis teste l'aile de son AC72 sur l'ex-Gitana XII, rallongé à 72 pieds.

    14 octobre, le trimaran aurait été acheté par John Sangmeister, un américain déjà propriétaire d’un Formule 40, celui-ci devrait s’aligner sur la Transpac 2013 avec entre autre Gino Morrelli, Cam Lewis, Jacques Vincent à bord.   22. 2025 Naufrage, perteVendu, nouveau propriétaire

    March, received from Jolly Holly: "Where is Tritium now? (2025)

    I’m happy to report that we have put nearly 2700 nautical smiles (no, that wasn’t a typo. ??) under her in the last six weeks and nearly 4000 NM in the last year.

    Our very first crossing, Was round point, conception, then a handful of Galavants through the Channel Islands, past Long Beach, San Diego, across the Mexican border and past the entire Baja peninsula, across the sea of Cortez, through Puerto Vallarta, and Acapulco followed by a beautiful Gulf of Tehuantepec crossing that took forever to get a weather window for, then on past Guatemala and El Salvador! The best part is we have only just began!

    Just over year ago, her future was looking quite bleak. After several pretty tough years for the whole world, starting in 2020, Tritium and racing understandably became less of a priority. She was donated to a nonprofit, and while all intentions were good, this little lady can be quite the handful. The boat wasn’t getting the attention she needed, and facilities to accommodate her dimensions can be quite costly on top of challenging to even find. Tritium began a downward spiral that eventually landed her in the beautiful town of Morro Bay. Racking up morning ball fees with the city and collecting seagull droppings her time was running short… when we first got involved with the harbor patrol and other personnel involved she was mere weeks away from having paperwork drawn up by the city to drag her on shore at the boat ramp, cut her into pieces and place her in a dumpster. A month later, just 5 days after I arrived to process final papers, THAT is exactly what happened to four other boats there in the harbor.

    It’s the sad fate of all too many beautiful “race horse”. They live a heyday, and once it’s all over, they go “behind the barn and off to the glue factory.” Myself having grown up racing actual horses had a passion for rescuing the special ones!

    As I became a young adult, that passion transferred into hot rods, and later in my life, when I discovered sailing, it continued on into the vessels I called home! After many other rescues, I discovered the boat I have had for the last 10 years sinking in Gulfport, St Pete. She was 2/3 underwater and in dire need. I floated her, pickled the engine, and proceeded to put 20k solo miles, plus crew miles on that boat over nine years. Long story short, I’m not your average girl, nor am I afraid of a challenge. After 14 years of living on the hook full-time cruising “normal” boats, I wanted a different flavor, and MY what a special “flavor” Tritium is!

    Life is short, more importantly, life is about experience!

    My husband and I have heard so much static about what we are doing with Tritium over the last year from disappointment of her being converted to a cruising boat all the way to “your gonna kill yourselves!” Well newsflash…she was VERY close to not existing at all, and i’ve been hearing that other negativity since I bought my first sailboat, having never even been on a sailing dinghy before. Soooo save your breath and try to enjoy the adventure for what it is.

    Honestly… In all of the crossings I’ve done, counties ive visited, and experiences I’ve had the most dangerous thing is when I return to the mainland, Rent-A-Car and am barreling down the highway at 70 miles an hour just feet away from other completely unpredictable 3000 pound speeding bullets. Something so many do every day without batting an eye. The fact of the matter is none of us are leaving this world alive????? so im choosing the full experience very aware of the consequences. At the same time, I am a FIRM believer that it does not matter how big you, your boat, or your balls are… Mother nature will always be bigger! She is Mi Jefa! No deadline or schedule will ever step foot aboard my vessel, other than assigned by her!

    I just wanted to give some substance to who I am as well as our purpose and mindset before continuing on the focus of this right up… which is Tritium getting the retirement she deserves!

    After sorting through a slew of messy paperwork and several scavenger hunt missions to reclaim her pieces from across California AND Nevada. We begin the planning of how to strategically fabricate living quarters and functional cruising equipment onto a very weight sensitive “canvas”… with nearly 18 years of marine engineering experience between the two of us, these challenges were some of the most exciting factors about this new adventure with Tritium. Starting with fabricating a custom dyneema suspended floating-roller anchor system followed by a Maxwell windlass, 5/16 chain, and 65lb manus! The reduction of the 14-foot pulpit and 120-foot head sail (will be recut to our J1) allowed us plenty of weight to accommodate this and a little more. No more midnight walkabouts for this noble steed! We also relocated the throttle controls for the engine, back to the port helm next to the windless controls and the newly installed Pelagic auto pilot… pulling Anchor to get underway is a dreamy push button task while sitting on your bum and gearing up to grind that mainsail to the moon!

    She’s had an incredible abundance of love and upgrades over the last 15 months now. An in-depth engine room overhaul, new transmission, and the addition of a shock absorbing coupler and folding prop. a good deal of work to the port rudder, new bearing, and shock in the center rudder, new tie rod ends WITH boots this time! 900w solar, 48v and 12v lifepo4 lithium systems, victron inverter, induction cooktop, full wet head with holding tank! Yes, we put through holes in the boat. And yes, they were all carbon fiber and properly vacuum bagged. We also installed a hatch on the fordeck over the head area after clearing several details of structural areas with the original manufacture of the boat. Built entire water system from scratch Bladders, New pump and we went with 3/8 plumbing as a constant awareness to weight calculations in our every accommodation, hot/cold showers cause who doesn’t love a 20 knott hot shower?

    and of course a watermake (18gph). All brand new switch panels to run said systems, brand, new AIS system. In between all of the fabrications have been constant catch up on neglected maintenance. Several new lines, blocks and rebuilt all of the winch grinder systems, gearboxes, and behind the scene magic to properly repair the functionality of this beautiful machine we are lucky enough to call home!

    So, for a more proper introduction, we are James and Jolly Holly, proud new owners and operators of SV Tritium! Tritium is well on her way back to her glory days with a new purpose in life! Sailing fast and living slow! We are back to full-time cruising and heading south towards Panama! (No, we’re not planning to go through the canal) Tritium will soon be getting a much needed hullout and bottom paint. We have also been peeling off this very tired vinyl and hopefully giving her a new facelift on the hulls, too! Just wait till you see the color we have picked!!! I could write a novel about the custom fabrication’s, repairs and upgrades we have made and will continue to make but I know the pictures and videos will shed a lot more light on the subject so I’m here to share the news! After four years of YouTube hiatus, we will be bringing the channel back and covering this dynamic Tritium adventure! We are actively editing footage but the first episode of how it all began is already up and the second one of the Anchor system fabrication is available on Patreon, but will air publicly on Wednesday! You are welcome to check out some of the pre-existing educational and entertaining videos including the salvage of a Jeanneau 54DS from several years ago at ‘SV Another Adventure’! Tritium also has an Instagram page under Sailing Tritium. Hope you enjoy the ride! Fairwinds and following seas!
