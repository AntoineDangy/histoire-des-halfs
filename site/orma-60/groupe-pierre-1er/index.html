  27. 2018 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en vente

    2018 Avril, For Sale ORMA 60, LAKOTA is for sale in Subic, Philippines, EUR 290,000 Tax Not Paid (US$ 350,175), In 2013, LAKOTA was sold to her current custodian who has turned her to fast cruising and transoceanic passage making. List of works since 2016: Stripped and sanded the boat entirely,on the hard stand for a full structural revision . Entirely re-painted the boat anew , same color except all decks and Topsides in White. Diesel Tank moved to rear , allowing refilling from outside. One bunk added forward. Some small, light corner shelves added. One lavac toilet added. Improved the access to rear cabin.

    One New fridge compressor added. One sea water pump added (in addition to the existing fresh water pump) Changed solar panels to lighter, semi-flexible ones. Changed the 2 X Garmin Chart plotters and Wind instruments on the Outriggers to new. Made a pair of own cradles for the boat; Changed all stays to new, from Gottifredi Maffioli in Italy. Changed all nets to new in 2018 from Docks de Keroman / Lorient. Changed all sails and Covers, Awnings and Trampoline protections : All sails New 2018 from Incidences La Rochelle in Hydranet 533 and Stormlite
