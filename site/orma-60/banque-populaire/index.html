  18. 2018 Refit, modification
    Devient Wai Hau

    Avril, BGYB is delighted to anounce the addition of the ORMA 60 trimaran WAI HAU to our Central Agengy listings !

    Using the Fujicolor trimaran moulds for her construction, Francis Joyon built this fast trimaran and won the 2000 OSTAR Europe 1 Race. After her race life, the boat received a lot of improvements to authorize her to be easily handled as a fast cruising trimaran but without losing her fantastic potential; Asking Price: 700 000 €; • A watch area has been created between the cockpit and the cabin • An electric retractable bow thruster has been installed and a self-deploying system for the anchor has been integrated in the transom together with an electric windlass. • The inside layout has been developed to accommodate 5 people to sleep. The folding table can offer 4/5 seats. In the galley corner, a sink with fresh water and a burner have been integrated. A manual toilet has also been installed. The main characteristics that make this boat unique compared to the other Orma’s 60 are: • A big volume in the main hull that make the boat more pleasant to cruise • The ability to manoeuvre in a marina with the help of the retractable bow thruster • A shorter mast (27.10 m instead of 31 m in the past) to make her easier to handle shorthanded, Year built / refit : 1994, Builder : Carmagnani Shipyard; Naval architect : Nigel Irens; Flag : Belgium, Location : Portugal, Price : 700 000 €, Length : 18.28 m; Beam : 15.80 m; Draft : 4.10 m; Material : Carbon & glass fiber Epoxy sandwich; Engine : 35 HP Lombardini; Fuel capacity : 82 L; Water capacity : 2 x 50 L L
