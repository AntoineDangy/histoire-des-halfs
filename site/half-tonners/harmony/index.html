  1. 1980 Construction, mise à l’eau

    Humphreys design No 23, construit chez RJ Priors and Sons, 9,42 x 3,16 x 1,77m. Red Cedar.

  2. 1980

    She was designed for Tony Allen (of Holt-Allen dinghy fame) and Mike Holmes

    Tour de l'Ile de Wight: ?e/?

    HALF TON CUP, Sandhamm: ?e/48, pas dans le coup

    It is fair to say that she was a more sensitive boat to sail than Roller Coaster and it took the crew a few seasons to get to grips with her. The Holt-Allen group had produced their own spar and sails and they too had a learning curve to climb.

  24. 2009 Vendu, nouveau propriétaire

    KYC Spring Series: 1er/??

    Sovereign's Cup: 1er/??

    ‘Harmony’ GBR5444 one of the most successful Rob Humphrey designs of 1979 was built for Tony Allen. Originally she had a striped livery similar to ‘Imp’. She was repainted and based on the East Coast for many years before being sold to an owner based in the Channel Islands. She was resold in 2006 to a North Wales owner and is now believed to be based in Ireland and renamed ‘Crewsaver Harmony’. (unknow source's name)
