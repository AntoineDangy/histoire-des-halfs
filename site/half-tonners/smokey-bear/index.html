    For sale: "Gunslinger", Stephan Jones 1978 built in 1978 is for sale offers around £12K. She is on the south coast U.K. he is a Hustler 32. Can be raced or cruised. Large boat for a half tonner. Condition is ready to go and she is more or less as built by Landamores in late 78 when she was built for the National Westminster Bank sailing club.   22. 2019 Mise en vente

    April, For Sale Ready to sail this season, this racer cruiser offers excellent sailing performance. The Hustler SJ 32 is a quality half ton off-shore racer with a reputation for being both seaworthy and comfortable. This particular boat "Gunslinger", has an interesting past competing in the 1979 Fastnet race. The cockpit has plenty of room and is well equipped whilst the wide and uncluttered decks are easy to move around. The current owner has conducted extensive work, vastly improving her condition. As well as being raced and used as a fast cruiser, this vessel has been lived upon for the past few years. She is spacious, has a full sized navigation table, berths for 7, extensive storage space and an easily accessible engine for maintenance. All in all, Gunslinger is a very capable racer/cruiser yacht that has had many practical improvements in recent years. Lying; Plymouth, Devon, UK, 10 000 £
