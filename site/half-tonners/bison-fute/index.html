  16. 2021 Mise en vente

    12 avril, site Halftonclasseurop, FOR SALE BEL-680 HALF MOON, Boat ready to sail and race. Half Moon has traveled the seas of Ré Island for 12 years and the owner has aged faster than his boat. It's time for Half Moon to start a new life. Half tonner designed by Jean Berret in 1978 Length: 9.7m – Beam: 3.17m – Draft: 1.79m (traditional IOR-keel) 2005 alloy Sparcraft mast & standding rigging 1996 Yanmar 2GM diesel engine Carbon spinnaker pole Dyneema runners & backstay 6 winches Sails: 2 mains, 2 genoas, 1 jib, 2 spinnakers Electronics : Raymarine ST60 (wind, depth, speed) on mast bracket and Raymarine fixed VHF. Cabin: Cozy but spartan interior containing full set of skai cushions and chart table with electric panel, battery status indicator & VHF. Visible St-Martin-de Ré. ASKING 10.000 EUR
