  9. 2022

    Comment from Facebook, "When Peter Norlin entered his design "Scampi" in the Around Gotland Race in 1969, he was completely unknown. Around Gotland Race is the main offshore event in the Baltic Sea. The previous year the Sparkman & Stephens design, the IW 31/ She 31, the Dufour Arpege 30 and Olle Enderlein One-off were the dominating boats competing for the Silver-ware. But when "Scampi" entered the scene, she completely out-permormed them all. She continued by winining the "Half-ton Cup that same year and also continnued winning the Half-ton Cup in international top competion, when following times competed in France and England. In the Half-ton Cup raced in England, the particular Scampi-class boat that won the event was the Scampi III. After the introduction of the Peter Norlin- design, the "Scampi", it was understood that the era of the classic designs was gone in offshore racing, at least for those aiming for top positions. Scampi changed it all."
