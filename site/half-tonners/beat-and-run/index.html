  1. 1980 Construction, mise à l’eau

  2. 1980 VictoireConstruction, mise à l’eau

    HALF TON CUP, Sandhamm: ?e/48, BERGSTROM

    'Beat and Run' Was built by a group of Swedish enthusiasts who wanted the win the HTC when it was in their home country. The built a boat for very strong winds which were not experienced in the event. She had some good races but was not good enough to win the event. (Info Vincent Delany 2011)
