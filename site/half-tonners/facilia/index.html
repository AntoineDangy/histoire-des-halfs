  6. 2022 Mise en vente

    For Sale Facil 30 1/2-ton - a cruel sailor and perfect family boat 95,000 kr 140,000 kr, We are now selling our beloved Facilia, an older racing boat that we have both competed in and family sailed with for over 20 years. The sailing properties are fantastically good. Facilia was built for the 1/2-ton WC in the mid 80's - The boat is a One off boat, built from the model Facil 30 but with a wider cockpit and flatter bottom. We have topped GPS speed 15 kn in a surf at Ölands Norra grund - Designed by Tommy Stål - Dimensions: 9.2 x 3.0 x 1.7; Defects: To avoid buyers looking for faults, we present here the parts we know about - The toilet has no extraction for tire emptying - The pump housing for the toilet may need to be replaced, some seawater leaks when pumping - In case of heavy rain, a few drops penetrate into two places in the salon - For a perfect bottom finish, a sanding is needed - Some fittings are a bit worn, but work flawlessly; Engine Diesel - Yanmar 1GM10 - Always starts like clockwork! - Dynematamp in the backrest (double backrest) - Autopilot ST-2000 Bruce anchor with Ankarolina and chain - New folding propeller from 2020 - Point with a bow ladder in the bow - Toilet with tank (not emptying tires) - New tire hatch for the forepeak from 2020 - Rudder with good bite. Turns on a fiver; - 6 berths + 2 hammocks - Forepeak for two people Lounge with two sofas to sleep in - Two knitting huts - Above the knitting huts there are also adjustable hammocks (perfect to sleep in while walking) - The salon's sofas can also be converted into sea berths - The cushions have removable upholstery that is easy to wash before each season. - Table in the salon that easily accommodates a large family If you have a small child, it works fine to also attach a hammock to the short end to the mast - Kitchen area with small sink, foot pump for salt water and fresh water respectively - Cabinets and drawers for storing kitchen utensils - Spirit kitchen, gimbal, two flames (nice to avoid gas, always worked very well) - Fresh water tank of 40 liters
