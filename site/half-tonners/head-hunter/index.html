    Janvier, For sale: Price £23,000 ONO, Hunter HB31RS production half tonner, believed to be one of only two RS vertions produced. 1984, epoxied from new. Good sail wardrobe including hardly used Sobstad genesis kevlar main, carbon spi pole. Nice interior much renewel & refitting in recent years. All usual instuments & bits etc.TCC .942. Also new Westaway carbon #1 subject to negotion.

  8. 2023 Mise en venteRefit, modification

    3 March Halftonclasseurop website, For Sale Headhunter - HB31RS 1984, Full refit 2016-2017: all new, Mills keel, Jeffa race rudder, Sparcraft rig, Craftsman 13 hp engine with saildrive, road trailer, complete deck, detachable bowsprit, central lift point, North sails J1,J2,J4, S1,5, S2,S3, A3. Full set crew wet weather gear, Zhijk Aeroshell in black with logo Headhunter GBR 5694, 9-10 suits from xxl to small. 2018: new J2, new FH main. Following HTC 2018, saildrive removed, light weight gearbox, carbon fibre p-bracket and fairing incl. seawater intake, new Gori prop, engine moved forward. 2019: new North S1,5, S2, J3, all never used, new in bags. All sails 3di race or raw. All very lightly used. All string dyneema. Currently stored indoors under cover on road trailer. GBP 40k or 45k EUR.
