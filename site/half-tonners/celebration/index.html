    For Sale: This racing 1981 SJ30 was built by Vervale to the one (sole) owner’s specification. Maintained to a high level this has been an extremely successful boat racing in IRC class mainly in Falmouth, Fowey and the South West. In recent years the owner has retired from racing and the boat has been lightly used by its crew. The original soft fittings (bunk cushions etc) were never placed on board but are available for cruising fit and are therefore in extremely good condition. The boat is now ashore in Falmouth in its own customised cradle. Always wintered ashore in compound. This boat appears in extremely good condition and totally belies its age. Arguably the best SJ30 in the country. The boat comes with an extensive sails wardrobe including sails purchased recently including a 2009 main and spinaker. The hulls finish is to racing spec and unblemished. The boat has no leaks above or below deck. An early inspection would be welcome and recommended. Cornwall UK GBP 16,500

    New owner: Paul Toms, Home port - Fowey, Cornwall, UK
