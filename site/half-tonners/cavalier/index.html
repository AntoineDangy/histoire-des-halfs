Chronologie détaillée

  1. 1975 Construction, mise à l’eau

    Plan Ron Holland, Golden Shamrock #03, Builder: South Coast Boatyard, She was built in Crosshaven, Ireland.

  2. 1975

    Isora race #7: 7e/9 B, S. TALBOT, Holyhead

  3. 1976

    ISORA, 4/9 races: 17e/55 B

  4. 1977

    ISORA, 4/7 races: 6e/22 B1

  5. 1978

    ISORA Race Week: 6 - 5 - 7 - 7 = 7e/17 B1

    1 July, Dun Laoghaire-Holyhead Race, 190NM: 9e/11 Class V, elapsed time 40h17', IOR: 21.7', coque rouge et blanche, J. TALBOT

    ISORA, 5/8 races: 6e/28 B1

  6. 1979

    ISORA, 2/7 races: DNC/28 B1

  7. 1980

    ISORA, 2/7 races: 20e/26 B1

  8. 1982

    ISORA, 3/7 races: 5 - 5 - dns - 4 - dns - dns = 8e/21 B1, J. TALBOT

  9. 1983

    ISORA, 2/7 races: 12e/18 B1

  10. 1984

    ISORA, 4/7 races: 8e/37 B1, J. TALBOT

  11. 1985

    ISORA Championship, 2/7 races: 15e/25 B1, J. TALBOT

  12. 1986

    ISORA Championship, 3/7 races: 15e/28 B1, J. TALBOT

  13. 1992 Vendu, nouveau propriétaire
    Devient Cavalier GBR 5107

    New owner, Bill COCHRAM, Club: Douglas Bay Yacht Club

  14. 2004

    Info Half Ton Europ: "The Golden Shamrock "Cavalier" is actually based in the Isle Of Man. Portsmouth Yardstick Handicap is 980, however Cavalier races competitively off 962. The boat belongs to my father and is still competitively raced. I now live in the British Virgin Islands so no longer race her, but I am going back to the UK in August to compete in Scotland's West Highland Yachting Week on her. I had a very enjoyable 12 years and 20000nm of racing/sailing on her."

  15. 2002

    Cork Week: ?e/??, COCHRAN, Douglas Bay Yacht Club/ Burntisland Sailing Club

  16. 2004

    1 August, Craobh to Oban Passage Race: 6e/14 Class 5, TCF: 0.700

  17. 2007 Vendu, nouveau propriétaire

    New owners: Bernard RATCLIFFE & Pauline SELLWOOD

  18. 2012

    November, mail received from Bernard Ratcliffe & Pauline Sellwood: "Cavalier is now based in Whitby on the North East Coast of England. We use her for day-sailing/racing and occasional trips up or down the coast. We have owned her for five years now."

  19. 2019

    April, Seen on Port Edgar Yacht Club list, (Edimburgh)
