  8. 1985

    MG HS30, Price: GBP 12,000, The vessel was manufactured by Marine Glass Boats on the Clyde as a racing yacht in the half ton class. She is now set up a s a fast cruising vessel ideal for inshore sailing. Two berths in saloon with an additional 2 quarterberths as required. The fore cabin could be used for an additional V berth but is currently used for the heads and as a sail locker.. The saloon has cushioned seating with new upholstery with plenty of storage space. he vessel comes complete with a good selection of sails inc 2*main, 2 * gib, 2 * spinnakers (to be confirmed ). Two good batteries, Alternator. Engine model: Yanmar, 18 HP
