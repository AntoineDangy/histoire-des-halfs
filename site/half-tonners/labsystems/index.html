  25. 2022 Mise en venteRefit, modification

    2 July, FOR SALE / VOGUE (SWE 9951) - Price reduced!!! SWE 9951 Vogue, ex FIN 5225 Fontana/Fonta. Design Eivind Still, built by Still Yachts 1986. Four boats where built 1985-1986 (FIN 4004, 5591 4646/4759) This boat Still built for himself. Kevlar reinforced. I´ve owned the boat for some 20 years, mostly for single handled cruising and some minor mostly inshore regattas. All the original trim is working. Original mast from Danish Nordic Mast. New deeper keel with a "foot" (not bulb) in 1994, lowering the point of gravity and increasing the righting moment. Some minor modifications made to make the cabin more comfortable, easily undone. Selftacking jib, easily removed. Furlex jib system, original forstay available. Sails are old and pretty worn. One spinnaker is good, heavy spinnaker is worn but works. Original engine, Volvo MD 2001, 9 hp fresh water cooled. Efficient Max-Prop feathering propeller Structurally the boat is in good condition, but a lot could be done to the finish. B&G wind data and Sonic speed (main unit serviced by B&G in UK last year, Sonic speed needs maintenance). Toilet with tank and through deck emptying (mandatory in the Baltic sea) Location: Stockholm area. Asking price 7.500 € or best offer.
