  7. 2022 Mise en vente

    For Sale 1990 IOR category 1/2 tonner race yacht. Designed by Daniel Andrieu of Jeanneau, and built by the S&C Purtell in Melbourne. Launched under the name Einstein, she competed in the 1990 Sydney to Hobart yacht race. We have been living on it for over a year now and it is well suited for that purpose, one of the cheapest costs for living in Hobart. We are only selling/moving out since we are travelling overseas.

    The cabin is a strip plank cedar and west system. The head is fully electric. Comes with two staysails, one headsail, spinnaker and one mainsail. 18HP inboard Diesel Volvo MD7B. Price AU $12,950 Negotiable, Length 30' - 9.14m, Vessel Location Derwent Park Tasmania, Hull Material Composite Foam core epoxy sheathed
