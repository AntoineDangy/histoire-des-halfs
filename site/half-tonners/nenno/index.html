Chronologie détaillée

  1. 1978 Construction, mise à l’eau

    Plan S. Jones, série: Hustler SJ32 modified, Builder: Landamores (UK)

    Green: informations from Half Ton Class Europe.

  2. 1979

    HALF TON CUP, Hollande: 8e/?. M. P. RICHARDSON

  3. 1996 Refit, modification

    She was found rotting in the mud on the river Medway, in a very tired and delapidated condition by the present owner, David EVANS.

    Over the years she has been extensively rebuilt and refitted, Only the GRP moldings and keel are original. Unlike most Half-tonners she is fully fitted out downstairs and has a double role as a comfortable cruising boat. She now also sports a big diesel on a sail-drive - no more horrible leaking hydraulic drive!

  4. 2003
    Devient Hullabaloo XV GBR 108

    HALF TON CLASSIC: 13 - 13 - (18) - 11 - 11 - (14) - 7 - 12,5 - 7 = 74,5pts, 10e/24. WFYC, David EVANS

  5. 2005 Avarie

    Avril: inscrit à la Half: NP. David EVANS

    31 mai, Port of Feliwtowe Regatta: 1 - 5 - 8 - 8 = 4e/7 half. coef IRC: 0,922

    Grave avarie de mât sur le favori local HULLABALOO XV.

    In 2005 the original in-line Procter rig eventually fell down, so the opportunity was taken to fit a modern 9/10ths swept spreader rig (no runners either).

  6. 2006 Victoire

    Port of Feliwtowe Regatta: 1 - 1 - 3 - (14) - 1 = 1er/16ttc et 1er/3 half. coef IRC: 0,917

    She is modified since then with a bigger rig - taller foretriangle now 9/10 instead of 3/4, also now has a double panel swept rig (22 1/2 deg) with the runners and checkstays removed! She is also faster for it!

  7. 2008 Victoire
