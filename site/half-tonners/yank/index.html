  24. 2023 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en vente

    15 February Halftonclasseurop website, For Sale Crakajax, a meticulusly maintained X-95 built in 1985 is for sale. Much to the Class' regret since the owners are keen sailors and regular participants of the Half Ton Classic Cup events... But as always, the advancing years take their toll... So this beautiful yacht is on the market again after 16 years of constantly upgrades. Interestingly she carries a much envied IRC rating of just 0.901! In the last years, apart from a new prop shaft, Gori-propeller and a new Corby designed rudder, she was upgraded with loads of new sails: 2022 light/medium carbon main, 2021 heavy carbon main, 2021 medium no.2 genoa, 2019 .6 spinnaker, 2018 light no.3 genoa, 2017 medium no.3 genoa, 2017 small no.3 genoa, She is fully equipped for both racing and cruising. She is seriously looking for new owners. Asking : 28.500 GBP - offers accepted
