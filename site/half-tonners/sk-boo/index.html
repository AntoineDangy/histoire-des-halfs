Chronologie détaillée

  1. 198? Construction, mise à l’eau

    Plan Chris BOYD, Puppeteer 330 modified, (Ian Howlett was also invovled in design), built by Chris Boyd of Puppeteer yachts Northern Ireland

    "...The construction was much lighter than the production model and she had a unique deck moulding with a tiny coachroof. She was a remarkable boat by being the lightest boat of her day and she excelled in light airs due to her low wetted surface area..." (unknow source's name)

  2. 1984 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireGrande course

    "Sk Boo was placed 1st or the top 3 in every race Belfast lough Strangford lough ISORA events Tomatin qualified for U K and Ireland half ton worlds, she had won class at Cowes week"

    HALF TON CUP, Troon, Ecosse: 18e/32, H. J. McDONALD, (bad offshore result)

    "Voile Magazine" avril 1985,

    Isora race #4, Holyhead-Howth: 13e/34 B1

    "...the boat was sold to Jim Pool but ..."
