  14. 2008 Mise en vente

    Owner Enda Connellan/ Terry Madigan

    For sale: "Space Odyssey", It was formerly known as "Ace" of Southampton and it had an excellent racing record. Now due to circumstances it has been laid up for the past couple of years and is now in need of both money and time to be spent to bring it back to a racing standard. The yacht is currently lying in Dublin, Ireland on a purpose built cradle. I look forward to hearing from anyone who would be interested in taking on this project.
