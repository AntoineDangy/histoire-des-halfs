  12. 2019 Mise en vente
    Devient Nuzulu F 918

    March, For Sale, Yacht "NuZulu" Stienman 31, $19,500 Negotiable, Fremantle, WA, NuZulu is a 9.66 mtr proven racing yacht. NuZulu has a strong racing background from Club Racing through to Sydney to Hobarts. NuZulu's IRC rating at .937 (2017) keeps her performing against many modern vessels.This yacht would be an ideal Inshore racer or on the Swan River. With a well balanced helm she is a delight to sail. Current Location Fremantle Sailing Club. Designer Kell Stienman, Builder Dodd Bros (Geelong Vic), Hull/ Deck Material : Divencell/Foam Sandwich, Engine Volvo Penta model 2003 Diesel 3 clylinder 28 hp V drive to 2 bladed Folding Prop. Fuel tank 40 lt SS. Has a leak from tank to Engine. 30 lt Water Bladder. Beam 3.17 mtr, Draft 1.87 mtr, Displacement 3000 kg, Keel : Lead with Bulb. Boom Cover, Accomodation : 4 berth with bunk cushions. Ground Tackle 2 Anchors, chain and warp to Cat 6 Safety. Safety: 6 Inflatable Life Jackets, wooden Plugs for all sea cocks, SS Lifelines, Navigation Lights, 2 Fire Extinguishers, Life Ring, Danbouy, V Distress Sheet, VHF Radio, Ephirb, 2 Manual Bilge Pumps, 2 electric Bilge Pumps. Sail Inventory : 1 x 2010 UK Sailmakers Matrix Main, 1 x UK sails 2003 Pentax Main for deliveries, 1 x UK Sails 2016 X drive Carbon /Polyester Laminate with Tafetta #1 Headsail, 1 x Uk sails Matrix 2010 # 1 Headsail, 2 x Uk sails Matrix with Tafetta # 3 (1 is almost as new), 1 x Light Symmetric Spinnaker , 1 x Heavy Symmetric Spinnaker , 2 Other Smaller spinnakers. Spars & Rigging : Tasker Aluminium Mast with Single swept back spreaders and boom 2002, Standing Rigging replaced December 2013, Aluminium Spinnaker Pole. MAST is Hinged to help lowering. Deck Gear : Harken Winward Sheeting Traveler 2015, 2 Cabin Top winches , 2 Primary Winches (All serviced 2017). Hull Was stripped back under water in 2013 Antifouled and Prop Speed October 2018
