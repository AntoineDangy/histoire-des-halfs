Lawless has competed in 4 Sydney to Hobart yacht races from 1982 to 1986 with the best result being 2nd in handicap in 1984 and has also competed in 2 Launceston to Hobart yacht races in 2010 and 2014. Under the ownership of current owner Steve McElwee Lawless preforms well under handicap in local club races.."
