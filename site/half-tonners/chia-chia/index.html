Chronologie détaillée

  1. 1984 Construction, mise à l’eau

    Plan Hugh Welbourn built by Green Marine, Carbon / Kevlar foam sandwich, Length over all: 8.95m, Beam: 3.20m, Draught: 1.70m

    Weight: 2556kg

    From Brenda Delany: Chia Chia' Designed by Hugh Welbourne. Originally had panda feet painted on hull below waterline. Original owner was Peter Whipp from Isle-of-Man, a regular competitor in the Commodores Cup with boats called 'Panda', He needed a race boat for a non- Commodore Cup year, so he built a half tonner.

    ‘Chia Chia’ GBR 584 is the only known Half Tonner designed by Hugh Welbourne
