  15. 2015 VictoireMise en vente

    Février, A Vendre... BLUES, famous one off halftonner from Håkan Södergren Build in 1980 - Kevlar/epoxy hull Keel changed in 2004 (Same keel as General Tapioca) Set of New sails (Used only for 7 days in 2011) NKE Electronics, with Navman plotter and Webasto heating system. Powerful Nanni 4-cyl Engine with 30hp (winner of the 'Envol Trophy' in Cowes...) Hempel epoxy primer finish on bottom Needs a little TLC, other wice ready to race. Laid up after 2011 Half Ton Cup. Boat is at Yachting Wave in Niewpoort, Belgium and can be visited there. 7500 Euros,
