    Seen on www.x-yachts.com/en/brokerage: For Sale A One Off Half Ton built for the Swedish Off Shore Worlds in Stockholm 1980. This is a sporty boat for a young sporty family or just for guys that want to race and have fun at sea at an affordable price. Boat in a good condition taking into consideration her age of 38 years You will be positively surprised when sailing her and seeing the interior. She is now winter-stored inside in a warm storage in Hanko about 90 minutes drive from the Helsinki airport or down town Helsinki. Also included is a winter storing cradle of steel? Designer Christian Bergenheim and Max Petrelius, Cabins 3, Berths 5, Hull Length 9.30 m 30'6", Beam (max) 3.20 m 10'6", Draft 1.70 m 5'7", Ballast - Std 2,700 kg 5,952 lbs, Displacement - Light 1,350 kg 2,976 lbs; Hull: Mahogany veneer and Epoxy moulded by vacum infusion and polyuretan varnish. This boat does NOT need the normal annual wooden boat treatments that normally are done. She is a fast sailing boat and the previous owner had her for 20 good years and has been very pleased but now he bought a big X-Yachts. The boat has a steel frame onto which the keel hs been mounted A sturdy construction similar to that of X-Yachts boats, Fiberglass deck, Mast height 14.5 meters, 2 Spreaders, 1.70 draft the keel weighs 1350kg,

  5. 2023

    March, from Facebook, "This is our 1980 Mach 1 half tonner. Definitely not landfill. Now converted to electric and used for cruising the Baltic Sea. Fun and lively little boat".
