  6. 2019 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en vente

    For sale "Samuel Pepys" has remained in the same ownership for the last 15 yrs with light use, only gently raced and wintered ashore. She benefits from considerable re-furbishment works and an excellent set of sails. Unfortunately, like many boats of her age, she has a dose of the pox and the asking price reflects this. She is currently drying out ashore, so you have the choice of continuing the dry out process or just launch and "go sailing" and treat in the future. She is far from about to sink and will provide years of entertainment in a lot of lovely boat for not a lot of money!, 17 540 Euros, lying: Great Britain, Swanwick;
