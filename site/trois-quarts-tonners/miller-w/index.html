    10 January, Cape Town to Rio Race, elapsed time 23d14h05': 2e/128 ttc, Dave COX

    From Facebook 2022 ""Cloud Nine" was also involved in a tragic incident - probably in '76? She was being delivered down the East coast of South Africa when, in a severe storm North of East London, a youngster was lost overboard. In attempting to recover him, the skipper was also lost overboard. The traumatised crew were taken off by a fishing boat, "Cloud Nine" was dismasted in the process and abandoned. She was found still afloat about a month later in the vicinity of Plettenberg Bay and towed back to Port Elizabeth."
