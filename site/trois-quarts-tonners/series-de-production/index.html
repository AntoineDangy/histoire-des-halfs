Yankee 38, Production model by Sparkman and Stephens. Built by Yankee Yachts Inc. (USA), the forerunner of the Swan 38’s. These vessels have breathtaking lines with handsome tumblehome, and have proven both to be winners on the race course, as well as superb family cruisers. They were heavily built hulls with longitudinal and athwartship stringers, a T-shaped cockpit with wheel steering, and an easily-handled rig. (NATALIA’S owner is her third, after purchasing her in 2007, and doing an extensive three year refit as documented above. Her second owner raced her extensively at the Detroit Yacht Club, having success in the Mackinaw Race series. NATALIA is the last of the 24 Yankee 38’s built. information from Internet 2020)
