    July, For Sale Supernova 3/4-ton Håkan Södergren, 36 Save SEK 120,000, Supernova 3/4-ton designed by Håkan Södergren. Manufactured at the Lidingö shipyard -83. A lot of documentation is available from previous owners, such as tuning manuals etc. In its early years, it has sailed very successfully and won the Gotland Runt and Kieler Woche two years in a row. Has undergone a renovation over the past two years, with the final touches remaining above deck. Either the windows are screwed there and the boat is ready to sail, or you sand and repaint the deck. All depending on the level of ambition. But it is up to you as a buyer how you want to do it. Info: - Freshly lacquered freeboard, De Ijssel color. - Renovated bottom, epoxy treated and very smooth. Bottom painted. - Sparcraft mast in very good condition, replaced about 20 years ago. 3 straight spreaders, backstay. New big fall. - New large from Boding (2 years old, sailed twice. Purchase price 54 600 SEK. - Lombardini engine with approx. 100 running hours. Model year 2015. Folding propeller, water-lubricated shaft seal. - Nexus nx2 system with 3 mast displays. Wind sensors. Garmin GND10. Nexus VHF. Simrad plotter. Raymarine plotter. - Diesel heater. - Alcohol stove. -Autopilot. - Miscellaneous foresails and spinnakers. - Tyresö cradle for winter storage. The purchase includes a 4x12 meter tent with a thick tarpaulin. Perfect to work in and protects the boat well.
