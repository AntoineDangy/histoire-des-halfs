    Devient Edge Of Disaster US 40527

    March, For Sale Hi-Tech Joubert Nouvelle Custom 33, Custom made Carbon Fiber racing sailboat. Built in Noank Connecticut in 1986 by Hi-Tech Boats (John Heinemann) Nomex core and all carbon Fiber construction. PHRF racing boat with a rating of around 104. Forte Carbon fiber mast new in 2011 and only used 3 or four seasons and Boat stored inside when not used. All halyards are high. Tech, Spectra or equivalent. Titanium shackles, Fractional rig, Spinnaker pole, Aluminum boom,Tiller steering, Lewmar 44’s three speed winches, Lewmar 40 ‘s (3) on cabin top , Harken traveler and blocks with wind war sheeting car. Nomex Honeycomb core with no bales core, keel stepped lead fin keel. Large forward hatch, two pipe berths. Tacktick wireless race master compass and wind shift indicator, speed and depth. Includes custom built trailer. LOA- 33 feet, max draft 6 feet, approx 6000 lb displacement. Sails are in good condition with one new custom North Spinnaker with giant Canadian maple leaf on it. Will consider financing. $10,000 or best offer!

    April, Comment from Facebook "The ex “Diamonds Noir” & “Menace” I’ve sailed against it a lot, very quick boat. $10K is very cheap. Check for delams in hull and deck."
