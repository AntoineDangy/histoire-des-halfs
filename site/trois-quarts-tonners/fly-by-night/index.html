    "Sydney Hobart Yacht Race, Official Programme",

    Comment from Facebook: "Designed for Western Australian yachtsman Ian Barron, Fly by Night was built in Fremantle in just six weeks, a frenetic burst intended to get the boat to the start of the Sydeny Hobart race. It was a windy Hobart and Fly by Night was forced to retire when her chainplate tie rod broke",
