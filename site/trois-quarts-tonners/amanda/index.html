  4. 2020 Mise en vente

    February, from Internet, For Sale, Beautiful and well maintained 3/4-ton in mahogany by Geraghty Marine from San Diego/USA (Design: Doug Peterson). Classic, bulky IOR lines and powerful 2 spreader rig by Reckmann. "Amanda" was originally built for the famous Swedish sailor Pehr G. Gyllenhammar and used for regattas. The current owner has had the deck reorganized and modified the yacht for fast cruising. Among other things, the keel weight was increased and the forestay was upgraded with a headsail furling system. Numerous investments in the past year include rebuilding the varnish of hull and deck, renewal of the bottompaint including epoxy primer, new standing and running rigging, new electronics, new coach house windows. Nanni replacement engine in 2006. All work was carried out by professional boatbuilders. "Amanda" is an eye catcher and in very good shape. Solid, classic interior in highgloss varnished teak with white painted panels. Location Kieler Förde, Kiel, Flag Germany, €49,000 VAT status EU-VAT paid

    Length 10.17 m, Beam 3.50 m, Draft 2.00 m, Displacement 3.9 t
