    For Sale Båttyp 3/4 Ton Byggnadsår 1974 Konstruktör Peter Norlin Varv Spillersboda Ort Norrtälje Löa 10,46 m Lwl 8,58 m Bredd 3,42 m Djupgående 1,85 m Deplacement 5,16 ton Material i skrov Kallbakat trä - The Swedish yacht designer Peter Norlin (1941-2012) produced many legendary, cruising and racing yacht designs including the ‘three-quarter ton’ class rating Amoress in 1975. Amoress , built by Lennart Minnegard & Arne Johnsson at Spillersboda Vara, Stockholm for Bo Andersson has a outstanding racing pedigree, participating in countless regattas and was, at the time the boat to beat, particularly upwind in heavy weather. Maintained in exceptional original condition Amoress is of cold moulded mahogany construction, bright finished inside and out, clearly showing her fine construction. A well built and presented three-quarter ton racer in long ownership used for annual family cruising and regatta sailing. Contact us for more images, information or an appointment to view.
