  25. 2019 Mise en vente

    February, Seen on AZAB Entrants list, Bert Janssen, doublehanded

    1 juin, AZAB: 29, elapsed 9d21'11' - ret = 45e/45 Overall, Bert JANSSEN

    Aout, For Sale - A Vendre, Type: HUSTLER 36 (Modified) Price: 17.000 EUR, 1980 Stephen Jones ¾-tonner 10.90mx3.40mx1.98m, Yanmar 2GM20 (professionally maintained) with new folding propeller (France Hélice), 2006 Z-Spars mast with 2 spreaders – no runners! Main (UK), Genoa 1 (North) + 2 (Elvstrom) + new 3&4 (Wittevrongel), storm & try sail, 1x masthead (All Purpose)+ 2x fractional spinnakers (All Purpose & UK), Carbon spinnaker boom, many new halyards, new sheets, new Lewmar 46 genoa winches, Cast iron keel with lower lead part + added lead bulb. All keel bolts replaced (May 2019), Nice interior with 8 berths – practical kitchen – new cooler – large chart table – marine toilet – large engine cover box serving as table & technical work space, 2x 75L flexible water tanks – 1x 80L diesel tank (new), New: 1x starter battery 110 Ah + 3x service batteries 110 Ah + Victron battery charger. Also new: 1x 50W Sunbeam solar panel + Victron Solar Controller for battery maintenance, New as well: shore power socket & new 15M 63A power cable with plugs, Raymarine ST60 speed-depth-wind, 2 autopilots (ST1000 tiller pilot + SPX 5 with electric linear drive & control display) + new Axiom chart plotter + new Em-Trak AIS transponder, New EPIRB Tron 60S + (older) Iridium phone, Compliant with the OSR Cat 1 (mono) – except the liferaft, Cockpit modified in 2017, with open transom. All electric wiring replaced (2019) by professional yard. CR, IRC & ORC weighed & measured 2018-2019 (IRC rating: 0,964), In June 2019 the boat did the AZAB-race (Falmouth – Azores – Falmouth). She’s your perfect club racer for crewed round-the-cans courses and short sea passages & for shorthanded racing and cruising.
