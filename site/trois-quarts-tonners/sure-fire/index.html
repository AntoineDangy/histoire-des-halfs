Chronologie détaillée

  1. 1987 Construction, mise à l’eau
    Baptisé Sure Fire KH 1234

    or 88 Plan Jones, Oyster SJ35

  2. 1988

    1 April, China Sea Race, Hong Kong to Manilla, elapsed time 110h33': 12e/44 IOR, Bart KIMMAN

  3. 1992

    17 April, China Sea Race, Hong Kong to Manilla: 42e/62, Bart KIMMAN

    Second owner, Toots Echauz (PH) (Commodore of the Manila Yacht Club), sale price reported as $28,000

  4. 1999

    Third owner, Isidro R. Consunji (PH)

    "Isidro R. Consunji went sailing on it only once, didn’t like it, and parked it for about a decade in Puerto Galera, Oriental Mindoro, where it was under the care of Caloy García, a founder of the Puerto Galera Yacht Club"

  5. 2009 Vendu, nouveau propriétaire

    Dry Dock, Victor Consunji had just bought the boat from his father, and had it brought to Manila Yacht Club to be repaired

  6. 2011

    20 septembre, fourth owners: Rafael Ongpin and Jérome Philippon, and new name,

  7. 2012
    Devient Surefire PHI 1234

    2012 Avril, Manilla Yacht Club,

    October,

  8. 2015

    Still sailing in Manilla

  9. 2021

    January, received from Rafael Ongpin/ Jerome Philippon, "Surefire regularly sails once or twice a month, racing in the Manila Yacht Club series. She has been to Corregidor and Subic." and all information about this boat.

  10. 2022

    February, Pictures from Facebook,
