    A Vendre-For Sale, NICHOLSON 33, lying Washington, 30 000 USD, Designed by Ron Holland for the Fasnet Race, this IOR boat is solid, sturdy, safe, extremely well built and makes a great distance racing boat and comfortable cruiser. Used for some light racing in mid-2000s, primarily used for cruising and day sailing. Yanmar 2GM20F (1994), less thn 100 hours per year since 2004; direct drive; new exhaust elbow and heat exchanger acid bath 2005; Maxprop 161/4" diameter. Fuel: 11 gal. aluminum, serviced in 2013. Water: 22 gal. aluminum. Holding: 15 gal. bladder. Kenyon propane fired 2 burner oven/stove; insulated icebox; manual marine toilet w/ macerator. New cabin cushions (foam and fabric) 2010. New electronics and electrical panel in 2005: Simrad speed, depth, wind; Standard Horizon VHF; AM/FM stereo/CD; Blue Sea Systems AC/DC panel. Battery charger installed 2009; new batteries and dedicated starter battery in 2009. Deck: 2 lewmar 44, three speed; 2 Harken 32, two speed; 1 Lewmar 16. 25Lb. CQR anchor with 30' chain plus rode. Harken roller furling. North Dacron/Mylar partial/full batten main, 2 reef pts (1994 - still good cruising sail); Staff Dacron 100% roller furling jib (2005 - still crisp); Wake Nylon 3/4 oz. rip stop spinnaker; new running rigging and Spectra halyards in 2006. Deck: 2 Contest bulkhead compasses, tiller steering, lines lead aft, hydraulic backstay, carbon fiber spinnaker pole, aluminum whisker pole, life sling, swim ladder, cockpit speakers, BBQ, BottomSiders custom cockpit cushions.
