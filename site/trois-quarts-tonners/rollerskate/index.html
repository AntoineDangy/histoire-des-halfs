  11. 2018 Mise en vente

    April, For Sale Custom Humphreys 3/4 Ton, Accommodations The ¾ Tonner offers spacious cabin with good headroom. Can sleep up to eight, including four permanent berths and up to four pipe cots ( one fitted and 3 provided loose). This is a cruiser/racer and the internal layout reflects this. The accommodation plan is as follows starting in the bow: Forepeak with stowage for sails and general equipment. Heads compartment with Lavac sea toilet and wash basin. Main saloon has setee berths port and starboard with lee cloths attached. Behind saloon berths is stowage. Galley on starboard side of companionway comprises of Gimballed single burner gas stove with oven and grill, Stainless steel sink with pressurised or manual water, plentiful storage for cutlery, pots and pans and provisions. On port side opposite galley is chart table with stowage and aft of this on both sides are quarter berths with lee cloths. Sails and Spars Rig by Proctor new in 1995. Keel stepped mast with twin in line spreaders. Slab reefing main boom with rod kicker. Twin luff headfoil and spinnaker pole. Stainless steel rod standing rigging, renewed September 2011. Sails 1 x No1 Aramid (Dolphin 2015 good condition) 1 x No3 Aramid (Dolphin 2013 good condition) 1 x Mainsail Aramid with internal taffeta (Dolphin 2013 good condition) 1 x Small heavy spinnaker ( Saturn 2012 good condition) 1 x Large spinnaker ( Hyde 2013 good condition) 1 x No4 polyester (Hyde 2015 as new) Mechanical Systems The ¾ Tonner is power by a single Yanmar 2GM 15hp two-cylinder diesel engine, shaft drive to a 2 bladed folding propeller. Cutlass bearing and inboard shaft bearing replaced 2017. Last out of water service, anodes, antifoul etc April 2017, Electrical Systems 2 x 12-volt batteries charging from the engine alternator. Deck Equipment 1 x Anchor Mainsail cover Navigation Equipment Compass Depth (TackTic) Speed/Log (TackTic) Wind direction (TackTic) VHF Radio VHF Handheld radio Navigation lights Safety Equipment Horseshoe lifebuoy Floating light Danbuoy Keel Lead Fin Keel History and Construction The hull and deck are a composite of glass matting and Kevlar with internal foam sandwich. Alloy frame at mast bulkhead and full length alloy tubular space frame. Semi balanced spade rudder with tiller steering and extension. Pushpit, pulpit, stanchions and all wires in stainless steel. There is a small bathing platform on transom with boarding ladder. £12,000 ($23,676), lying Ramsgate,
