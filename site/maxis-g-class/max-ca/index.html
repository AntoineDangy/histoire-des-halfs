  25. 2016 RecordVendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en venteRefit, modification

    Décembre, For Sale 1984 Multiplast Racing, Length: 74', Engine/Fuel Type: Twin / diesel, Located In: France, Hull Material: Fibreglass/GRP, Current Price: EUR 325,000 Tax Not Paid (£ 276,972)

    Racing catamaran 74 ft - 1984 - Totoal refit in 2010, Refit in 2010 for the Route du Rhum, this racing catamaran in carbon and fiberglass is a rare units on the market. This unit was built in Multiplast Shipyard and the architect is Gilles Ollier an bought in 1986 by Bruno Peyron. She was Atlantic crossing record holder twice. This cruising catamaran is in Excellent Condition
