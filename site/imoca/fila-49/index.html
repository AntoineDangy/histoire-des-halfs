  13. 2008 RecordGrande course

    Octobre: This high tech, composite ocean racer, "Saga Insurance", as "Fila" took a 1st in the Around Alone '98/99 with Giovanni Soldini in Class I. This Open 60 set a new record for a distance sailed in a single-handed monohull in 24 hours. With her canting keel, twin daggerboards and rotating mast, the IMOCA Open 60 "Saga Insurance" is very competitive. Located in United Kingdom EUR 440,000
