  20. 2012 Mise en vente

    Mars, For Sale, Still a modern boat. The boat, inspite of its age (relative to the majority of the current IMOCA class fleet) has always been at the forefront of modern design and able to be transformed to conform to the increasingly stringent race regulations and technological developments from the organisers. In addition to the various changes brought about, most notably by Yves Parlier when he sailed this boat, she underwent a complete overhaul for Yannick Bestaven to achieve his goal: to participate in a Vendée Globe without any fossil fuels onboard. Flag: French, Location: West France, Price: 150 000 €
