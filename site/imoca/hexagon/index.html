    plan Merfyn Owen Group. Largeur: 5,5m TE: 4,5m; mat de 27m, surface Voiles: 165m2, construit par Souther Ocean à Tauranga en NZ.

    Différences entre Kingfisher et HSBC: un mat plus petit alors que la surface de voile est plus grande, une seule dérive, la présence d'un tangon orientable. Et le cockpit est doté de deux barres à roue et s'ouvre sur l'arriére.

  13. 2010 Refit, modification
    Devient Rusalka

    Winter, in London: full refit to turn the solo offshore racing yacht in to an offshore Med racer that could easily be handled by the owner and a crew of four. Modifications included a new engine, a modified forward bulkhead and new chainplates, and the unusual inclusion for an Open 60 of basic a galley, a shower and mountings for a chemical toilet.

  14. 2011 Mise en venteRefit, modification

    Avril, site Owen Clark: For sale: Rusalka, ex Artemis 1, ex Pindar and originally built as Hexagon is reluctantly for sale by her owner having been purchased for use as a private yacht in late 2009. Rusalka is a swing keel water ballasted IMOCA Open 60 and is one of the lightest of any generation ever built. Even with the increased weight of modifications to her rig bringing her in line with later Owen Clarke designs she still measures in at 8,500kg. Rusalka has been continually professionally maintained and updated throughout her life by her owners/skippers including Mike (Moose) Sanderson. As a result, she's still a very fast Open 60 and is also the last of the Open 60's built that would have been valid for the Eco 60 class in the VELUX 5 OCEANS RACE 2010-11

  22. 2017 Mise en venteRefit, modification

    January, For Sale Open 60 Rosalba, Built as Hexagon HSBC for Graham Dalton to sail in the 2002 Around Alone, After that race, the boat was purchased by Pindar for Emma Richards. In consultation with the Owen Clarke Design office significant development was undertaken with Mike Sanderson as skipper. This included a conversion from single central daggerboard to twin asymmetric boards, and conversion of rig to removable genoa with halyard lock system -now common amongst the fleet. Between 2006 and 2009 the boat was owned and managed by Artemis Ocean Racing. She was originally used as the race boat competing in the 2006 Route de Rhum and 2007 TJV. She was subsequently used as the corporate boat and was RORC Class Champion in 2008. In 2010 the boat was purchased by the previous owner and renamed Rusalka. The boat went through an extensive refit during which; in addition to general servicing, the decks, topsides, bottom and appendages were re-sprayed and the keel modified. Hydraulics overhauled, engine and saildrive replaced, interior redesigned and galley fitted, and much more. Under current ownership the boat was renamed Rosalba and has been impeccably maintained and further invested in with new rigging, electronics, mainsail, genoa, code 0 and J2 and an overhaul of keel hydraulics. She has also been fully coded for charter activity. Location: Genoa Italy, 395 000 Euros, VAT payed

  24. 2018 Vendu, nouveau propriétaireMise en venteSponsorRefit, modification

    Janvier, un français (de plus de 40 ans) avait mis une option sur le bateau en vue d'une participation à la RduR 2018 et au Vendée Globe 2020, puis l'a annulée (Info Tips & Shaft juillet 2018)

    12 janvier, info "Tips & Shaft", Richard Tolkien actuel propriétaire l'a remis en vente

    For Sale 2002 Custom IMOCA 60, US$ 340,425, Under new ownership In 2014 a further cosmetic refit was carried out to the hull bottom and topsides with a re-fair and antifoul to the hull bottom and the topsides vinyl wrapped (now removed) All the future fibres PBO rigging was replaced with the Karver furlers serviced and load tested in 2015. At the same time a complete overhaul of the electronics resulted in a new B&G chart plotter and computer system, AIS, extra keel controls by helm, new Sat dome etc. The keel was ultra-sounded in March 2014. Another very comprehensive survey was undertaken on the vessel in 2017 when purchased by the present owner. New Future Fibres forestay and backstays were fitted in 2017 along with the blue vinyl wrap being removed revealing pristine white Awlgrip topsides, in addition a full anti-fouling refresh was carried out with the rudders and keel additionally painted hi vis yellow. Lightly used by previous owner 2015- 2016 (mostly day sailing, but also competing in the Fastnet, and Middle Sea Race). Sailed by current owner and crew from Naples to Caen September 2017. Full (500 hours) engine and sail-drive service and new Gori race propeller fitted, Cascais, October 2017. IRC rating 1.68, Boat and rig stored undercover at V1D2 yard, Caen, Northern France, Viewing by appointment only

    21 décembre, bateau en chantier pour être de nouveau à la jauge Imoca (quille), Richard Tolkien cherche des partenaires pour la saison 2019 (mais ne parle plus du Vendée Globe), (Info Tip&Shaft)
