  13. 2009 AvarieMise en venteRefit, modification

    June, IMOCA Open 60 - Aviva 1 for sale. On stepping aboard this older generation Open 60 it is clear she has had a very well maintained past and wisely spent budget. Originally 'Team Group 4' under Mike Golding she then completed the 2004/5 Vendee Globe as 'Hellomoto' under Conrad Humphreys backed by Motorola, she then benefited from a full refit in 2006/7 as 'Aviva' under Dee Caffari. The yacht has been well maintained with no expense spared. She is in good condition with little visible damage or signs of wear and tear to the areas where one might otherwise expect it. The yacht lost its rig during the 2007 B to B. We have sourced a possible replacement, but the vessel is for sale without a rig. She is an eligible contender for the Eco division in the VELUX 5 OCEANS RACE 2010-11 , Asking Price: 100,000 GBP, Lying: South Coast UK

  15. 2010 AvarieVendu, nouveau propriétaire
    Devient OZ AUS 60

    Garry a racheté le mât de l’ancien Hugo Boss d’Alex Thomson, ex Pindar, pour remplacer celui cassé lors de la Transat Ecover BtoB en 2007. Ce mât étant un des plus grands jamais gréés sur un Open 60, il prévoit de le réduire de quelques mètres pour se conformer aux règles de la course.

    19 aout, Two Velux 5 Oceans launches, Garry Golding and Brad van Liew relaunch their Eco 60s. Golding added: “Getting the boat back in the water is a real milestone for us. It's been really hard work but she looks amazing. We are really proud of Oz and are very grateful to everyone who has helped get her this far.”

    6 octobre, Velux 5 Océans, l’Australien Garry Golding renonce, Il n’est pas qualifié pour la course et n’est toujours pas arrivé à La Rochelle.
