  31. 2016 The long Offshore Race turned out to be a rather uninteresting beam reach to Cherbourg and back. Through smarter navigation we managed to round the buoy outside Cherbourg first. On the beam reach back to Weymouth we outperformed the competition and left the fleet behind the horizon, only to get stuck becalmed off Portland Bill. Then the rest of the fleet, led by Laurent Cordelle in his Ecume de Mer, read the situation and circumnavigated the becalmed area by letting the tidal current carry them in a wide circle around us and finally scoring. Robber finished 4th in the race and 4th overall. — Robber · skipper
