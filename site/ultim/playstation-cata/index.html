  13. 2011 Record
    Devient Virgin Oceanic

    6 avril, Still, the late Steve Fossett’s former outright RTW record-holding Cheyenne/Playstation is back to work, this time as the tender to Richard Branson’s new record-smashing deep flying sub concept. Branson said that much of this was actually Fossett’s idea. From Virgin Oceanic: Fossett had conceived of a record-setting solo submarine dive to the bottom of the Mariana Trench in the Pacific Ocean. The project would be supported by converting his 125' racing catamaran, Cheyenne, into the mothership and support vessel. It was only fitting since Cheyenne was built to (and did) shatter sailing speed records. Now she would help break another record. Chris was instantly captivated and set his sights on completing Steve’s mission to the deepest part of the planet. He also expanded the project from a single solo dive to the current goal of five oceans, five dives, exploring the deepest depths in each of the five oceans of the world. Cheyenne will actually haul the sub on special slings under her tramps, bringing her to un-Cheyenne like levels of performance. She certainly looks better like this than as the Morning Light camera cat, though! (Info Sailing Anarchy)

  19. 2026

    Avril, Troisième vie en vue pour l'ex Playstation, ex Cheyenne de Steve Fosset ? Après la chasse au records océaniques à la voile, après plateforme de tournage cinématographique, de recherche de trésors sous marin, de tests aile volante, la plateforme pourrait être transformée en drone, par la société Saildrone Inc sur la base navale d'Alameda. Base où le catamaran a été remorqué il y a quelques semaines depuis Richmond, toujours en baie de San Francisco.
