2011 24 novembre: For Sale, "SUPERHERO", ex JHN & EPJ4. Buil 1988 in carbon hull. new IRC keel fitted in 2000. Numerous wins including Cork Week and Around The Island Race. Boat in good conditions. Quoted by the experts as one of the best half tonners ever built. Current IRC 0.948. GM 2 16HP Engine.
Décembre, Toni STOSCHEK and Janne TULOKAS from Finland (also the owner’s of halftonner “Blues”) just bought this halftonner. “Superhero” will be lying in Nieuwpoort, Belgium next year.
