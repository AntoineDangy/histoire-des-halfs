Plan Doug PETERSON Design #73, "CONRAD 25" ou "CONRAD 760" construit en Pologne:

 

From Ivan Ivanovsky, owner of Conrad-25R "Ulan": "My boat is Conrad-25R, was built in Poland in the Conrad Shipyards in 1984 to design Doug Peterson, serial number 147.

Boats of the these project were in 3 versions: racing (Conrad-25R) and racing-cruising with another section of the deck (Conrad-25RT and Conrad-25T). Conrad-25R and the Conrad-25RT have a mast 7 / 8, Conrad-25T had a corpus of Conrad-25RT reduced mast to mount stay at the top of the mast. In some European countries, these boats were delivered under the name of Peterson-25 or Conrad-760.

In 1979-1990 years, these boats were delivered in large quantities in the USSR. There is a yacht with serial numbers greater than 320. The prototype of these boats is the winner of Quarter ton cup 1978 Magician V, changed the serial quarter tonner Yamaha-25."

There is a class association website www.25ft.org.
