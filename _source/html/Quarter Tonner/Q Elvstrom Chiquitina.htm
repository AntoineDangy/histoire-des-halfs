Chiquitina, K 7171
à jour au: 2022
1977 plan Elvstrom, built by Fibrester, Spain

 

1977 First owner, Rodney Pattison.

1980 Sold to Netherlands

Chiquitina, NED 8864

1993 New owner.

2014 Pictures and informations from Dutch QT Facebook: "The current Dutch owner bought her 21 years ago in Southampton. Double Olympic gold medalist Rodney Pattisson was the first owner. We also received the old IOR certificate from the RORC Rating Office. 'Chiquitina' is one of the two Elvstrøm 1/4 ton boats in the Netherlands, the other one is called 'Femme Fatale' (G 1749)."

2018 14 septembre, Zuiderzee Regatta: (7) - 7 - 6 - 6 - 7 - 6 - 6 - 7 = 6e/8 QT, André VINK
Picture and information from Dutch QT Facebook, Rodney Pattison: "Delighted to hear about your ¼ Ton Cup revival. Yes, I did originally own 'Chiquitina' and understand she has been beautifully restored in Holland. In 1977 I bought her at the Spanish boatyard called Fibrester near Barcelona and trailed her to the UK. I really liked her flush decked lines. She was/is a beautiful looking boat, probably the prettiest of all the Quarter Tonners! I removed the rudder skeg and fitted a balanced rudder instead which improved tacking and manoeuvrability considerably. I sold her to the Netherlands in 1980"
