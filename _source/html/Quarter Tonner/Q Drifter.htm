Drifter 25,

Drifter 25 designed by Leen Hoogmoed.
Two Drifter 25s ("Driftbui" & "Hades") competed in the 1977 QT Cup in Helsinki and ended somewhere in the middle.

From sailboatdata.com: LOA: 7.45 m LWL: 6.30 m S.A. (reported): 32.20 m² Beam: 2.75 m Displacement: 1,250 kg Ballast: 620 kg Max Draft: 1.48 m Construction: FG Ballast Type: Cast iron First Built: 1976 Last Built: 1982 # Built: 35 Builder: F. Dekker shipyard (Amsterdam),

Designed under IOR quarter ton rule, the prototype 'Weer 's Zeilen' (H-2313) won the Dutch Championships in IOR Class VII in 1976.
