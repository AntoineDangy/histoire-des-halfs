2009 May from forum: "We know at least one of the three original Peterson/Eichenlaub pin tail QTs (Valkyrie) was cut up a few years ago. No one seems to know where the other two are ("Tickled Pink" and "El Principio")"; 

2018 Information from Dutch QT Facebook: "TICKLED PINK, Peterson Quarter Ton, Design #12, One of the original Eichenlaub builds, here being finished at Carl's yard. Twice runner up in the Quarter Ton North Americans ('73 & '74), A PINK boat! Whattha?, Note the visitor riding in on his cool big/little stingray!" "While GANBARE was a small, light One Ton, these QTs done at the same time were big and heavy for the class. The last I heard Tickled Pink was sitting in a yard in Ohio Deteriorating. Like so many good ole boats. Raced Wizard for many seasons. Remember one particular run with blooper up. We crashed so hard the water was inches from the companionway. Thought she was done!"
October, Information from Dutch QT Facebook: "'Tickled Pink', a Doug Peterson 1/4 tonner (Design #12). She was one of the three original Peterson / Eichenlaub pintail 1/4 ton boats. ‘Valkyrie’ was cut up a few years ago... No one seems to know where the other two 'El Principio' and 'Tickled Pink' are..."
