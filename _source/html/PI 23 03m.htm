Half Ton Classic Cup 2023: elle aura lieu en Norvége pour fêter les 40 ans de la HTC 1983 qui avait eu lieu dans ce pays. De nombreux halfs norvégiens ont été remis en état pour l'occasion, d'autres ont été acquis pour y participer. 35 bateaux sont déja inscrits, voir le trexte d'introction du site ci dessous:

From https://www.htcnorway.no/HHTCC/: "We look forward to welcoming all sailors for a fantastic week at Hankø. The Hankø Half Ton Classics Cup marks the 40 years reunion of the world championship at Hankø in 1983. In mid-February 2023 - 7 months before the championship - 35 boats are already registered with crews from Norway, Sweden, Ireland, England, Belgium and Brazil. This bodes well and we are now aiming for as many as 50 1/2 ton boats and more than 250 sailors will be ready for the championship in August. Since late summer 2020, the 1/2 ton boats have received their well-deserved racing comeback in Norway. Old boats have been brought in from all over the country and collected in Skjeberg. Here many of the boats have been restored by boat builder Martin Kamperhaug and are now in the same or better condition than when they were new. Welcome!"

 

End of March, received from Kaspar, OT Holland "Golden Apple" new owner this request ""It is my intention to next year rejoin many of the 1974 1-ton participants, for a 50-years celebration for "Gumboots", in Travemünde, during "Travemünder Woche". Would you have contact details to some of those 1-ton boats, which raced in 1974?"
