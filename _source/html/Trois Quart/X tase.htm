2022 June For sale X-102 - Xtase, 275 000 kr, Building number 129 1985 Classic X-yacht is sold ready for spring and to that extent ready for sailing. In recent years, the boat has been used extensively for both shorthanded racing and lots of touring sailing, and is therefore very well equipped for all types of sailing. She has a new mainsail and genoa from 2019, genaker and code 0 on a roll, as well as two players. Of the instruments, there is a B&G 7 "navigator from 2019, Raymarine autopilot, log, weight and wind instruments. A webasto diesel boiler has been installed which works really well. See also inventory list for the boat's other equipment. The engine has been switched to a "switch" in 2017, ie. a used saltwater cooled similar to the original, but newer and in good condition, and it runs perfectly. Blackjack and shaft bearings have been changed. The propeller is a 2-blade max-prop, ie. has a really good grip, both in front and back. Inside, she is marked by age and an owner who has most wanted to sail and less time to sand and varnish. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BB796QtPprQ The bottom is completely renewed in May 2022, first by sandblasting the entire bottom, then plastered with Hempel Pro Filler and sealed with 7 coats of primer with International VC TAR2, and finished with bottom paint. The keel is extra primed with Owatrol CIP primer. Primer Hempel Mille.
