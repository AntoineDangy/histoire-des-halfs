Abu Dhabi to complete qualifier, 111 more miles to sail for the Ian Walker-skippered VO70. Abu Dhabi Ocean Racing have agreed to sail a further 111 miles to complete their qualification for the Volvo Ocean Race 2011-12 following their retirement from a test race over the weekend. During the 360-mile qualifying race from Alicante to Palma, Mallorca and back, the six boats were required to carry out several safety drills. Having completed all of the required procedures, Abu Dhabi opted to motor rather than sail during a windless section of the course.
