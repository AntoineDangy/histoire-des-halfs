October "Seahorse", For Sale Imoca 60 Gentoo. 725,000 ex VAT GBP. Located in Gosport, UK. Recently race prepared for skipper James Harayda, Gentoo is in great shape for the next Vendee Globe Series culminating in 2028. Originally designed for Alex Thomson by Finot-Conq, the beamy and powerful 'Hugo Boss' finished 2nd in the Barcelona World Race in 2008. This boat has been continually optimised including a comprehensive refit in 2018 with the addition of foils, new coach roof, structural reinforcements and new ballast configuration. All systems have been serviced annually including NDT of mast and keel; the boat is fully IMOCA class certified 2024. Further upgrades to the boat include new standing rigging, mainsail, J2, J3, and small Gennaker (2024), rudder bearings & Mastervolt system (2023).
