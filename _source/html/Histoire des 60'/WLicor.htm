"However, after making the youngster happy for another season of navigation, "Laisvë" was left ashore again for major repairs. Because the metal shell of the ship, which is sensitive to corrosion, was badly corroded by the sea salts instead; during the welding work, some structural defects left by the shipbuilders themselves came to light. In addition, it is necessary to replace the piping and complex electrical wiring inside, after removing the interior trim. In other words, it is not enough to repair the ship temporarily - it needs to be thoroughly repaired. Such repairs are expensive; due to lack of funds, the accelerated work stopped.
