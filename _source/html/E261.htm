Igor Sidenko, SID 928 half-tonner

This project is already a serious half-ton class sailing vessel. The author of the project is Sidenko Igor Igorevich. It was produced simultaneously by 3 firms: this is the Tallinn Shipyard, in the noble city of Yaroslavl, Mr. Moskvin and our company. At the moment we are left alone. (Pictures from Internet 2009)
The boat is designed for long voyages, races (drawn under the IOR). The boat has a typical half-ton layout and is designed for a crew of 6 people. The vessel itself is very nautical, a good tacker, has a good run. We know that there was a transition around Europe - the boat was ferried from St. Petersburg to Sochi."
