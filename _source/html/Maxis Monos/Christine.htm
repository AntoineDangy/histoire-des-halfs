Comment from Facebook 2022 "Built by Fred Preiss behind his used Auto business. Took him a couple of years to build her. Designed by Dick Carter as far as lines go...structural basics also by Dick, but Fred was a pretty good engineer and modified some things. She was cold molded using West Systems. Fred designed and built all the hardware including the coffee grinders. They worked OK until Big Boat series in 1980. If I recall correctly we finished that series with only one working primary winch as we had to scavenge parts from the other one to keep one working. I sailed with Fred and his crews numerous times including BBS. At that time I was with Hood Sailmakers and we built her original sail inventory. She was pretty fast but really suffered from lack of funds to compete with the likes of Kialoa III and Windward Passage. She had a very limited sail wardrobe. Many people were negative about Fred's efforts, however I think he deserves credit for tackling a project like this and pulling it together with a reasonable degree of success. He was quite an innovative thinker."

The intellectual property of professional naval architecture is the work of Dick Carter. In 1977 it was formally launched with triumph at Redondo Beach Ca and after sea trials and details were handed over to the owner who commissioned it. Fred Preiss has completed his new construction of sailing yachts in the United States. The central hull was built with wood/GRP (cold pressed) composite. The component of the superstructure of the sailboat is mainly made of composite. It has a fairly deep draft of 4.3 m (13 ft). In 1998 it had carried out maintenance and modification and refit.
