SILVER SHAMROCK, GBR 8832
2004 David Carr
Additional information: this boat is the one that Harold Cudmore won the 1/2 ton cup in Italy 1976 and then sailed down the grand canal in Venice to celebrate their victory!

2010 Ron Holland's 1/2 ton 1976 World Cup winner, always achieved good results. Owner retired from racing ten years ago and not raced since. Maintained in excellent condition. Good inventory including ST50 & 60 instruments, autohelm, GPS. Hydraulic backstay and vang. 2010 new rigging and Tuff Luff foil, recent rudder and 2010 new bearings. Large sail wardrobe with two new, unused spinnakers. Six berths, two doubles, two singles and two pipe cots.
Engine Yanmar 12YSE, totally reconditioned in 2002 approx, starts instantly every time, afloat Falmouth. Winter layup at Penzance from mid Oct. Phone 01736 796 176
