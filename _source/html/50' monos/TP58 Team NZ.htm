2008 For the first time Team New Zealand will have a TP52 in their own colours next season. A Botin Carkeek design is already under way at Cookson’s in Auckland. The plan is to have the boat in the water around the time of the Louis Vuitton Pacific Series (31 January to 14th February) before shipping it to Europe
