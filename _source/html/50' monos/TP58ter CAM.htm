The boat was built in 2009 by Cookson, sharing the mould of ETNZ TP52. It was engineered by Pure NZ and we re-designed a new flush deck to reduce the VCG and windage. This boat also has a taller mast than a TP52 and its much lighter and stiffer than its competitors."
