December, Andy Middleton chartered and raced this yacht last year as GYR Wot Eva, but retired and is back chartering the same yacht, renaming her Performance Racing. Since 2010, Sailors With Disabilities (SWD) has raced and trained on this TP52, (the former Yassou, Glory and Wot Yot), kindly donated to SWD by wotif.com co-founder Graeme Wood, who contested four Hobarts (2006,2007, 2008, 2010) with it. SWD, led by David Pescud, finished the 2014 Rolex Sydney Hobart 47th overall in IRC and eighth in Division 1 with this boat and PHS 16th in 2012.

2021 July "Seahorse", For Sale 2001 TP52 "Wot Eva". 220000 AU. Located in Sydney, Australia. This TP52 is of that generation that were built for offshore racing and by the highly regarded US builders Goetz. Originally called "Yassou" it then became "Glory" and was converted from symmetric spinnakers to assys before being brought to Australia as "Wot Yot" and campaigned at a high lever before being donated to the Making Waves Foundation and renamed "Wot Eva..."
