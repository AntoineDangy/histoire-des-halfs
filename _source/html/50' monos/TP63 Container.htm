For Sale, CONTAINER is the latest cutting edge TP 52 in the world today. Designed by Judel/ Vrolijk and built by Green Marine, CONTAINER was commisioned for the 2011 TP 52 Med Cup and is race ready for the 2012 Med Cup. She has the potential to be an unparralled IRC winning machine. CONTAINER is offered complete with three sets of North sails, all having low documented hours. The boat has a High Modulus three-spreader mast and boom, top of the line B&G race package, several sets of running rigging, Caraboni hydraulics, custom Harken winch package and plenty of useful spares. This event winning TP52 is in immaculate condition as she has been shed stored in Valencia, Spain. CONTAINER is offered at 900,000 Euros however the owner is eager to see her move.
