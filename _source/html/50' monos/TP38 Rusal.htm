This is clean slate providing the opportunity to complete an IRC conversion to suit your sailing needs. As seen with sister ships Samba Pa Ti and Windquest, this generation Botin & Carkeek designed TP52 offers an fantastic platform for an IRC conversion and some spectacular power reaching and running performance. Synergy is a 2006 Botin & Carkeek TP52, built by Latini Marine. This TP52 was delivered for the 2006 Med Cup Series and has competed in the Mediterranean and in the USA for the past 2 years. The design and construction is very robust as compared to the modern trend in the TP52 class currently racing in Europe. This is a 3rd generation TP52 designs from the very successful Botin & Carkeek office, This TP52 is currently optimised for the Breitling Med Cup series, competing in 2006 & 2007 and is still in TP52 class mode. Her sister ships, Samba Pa Ti and Windquest (former Warpath) have proven to be a very successful IRC boat. Synergy has just completed the Ft Lauderdale to Key West Race and Key West Race Week .
