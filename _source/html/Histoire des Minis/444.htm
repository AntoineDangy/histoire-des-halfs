"It is a good class. Classe Mini is the best class I have ever ever been in. it is very French, but the Mini is very different to the Figaro. The Figaro is very secretive, whereas the Mini is very open. I can walk along the dock and talk to anyone and you can chat about the weather and the routing. We talk about sail shapes. It is one big pool of information and we all chip in and discuss it. There is no top secrets like a dinghy class. That is a good thing. It may be very French but if you look at the entry list the number of foreigners in the entry list is very high. For an English person, most of the French speak English and after two years you end up picking up some French."
