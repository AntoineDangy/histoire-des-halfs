I am selling my Mini 6.50 Proto No. 326 (aka. Classe Zero or Mini Transat). After couple of years of regattas and coastal sailing in Norway, I am looking for a family -friendly cruiser and must therefore sadly sell my little pocket rocket. No. 326 planes already in 8.4kts and can reach blistering speeds close to 20kts in the right downwind conditions. Once you set a gennaker or the Code0, there's no stopping her. As she only weighs 950kg, any wind conditions with very little wind, the Mini glides well away from the bigger yachts. During my ownership (since spring 2021), I have upgraded the instruments comprehensively and done a complete changeout of the battery and charger systems. Everything works outstandingly onboard with all instruments all connected together. Autopilot can sail on both course and wind, VHF with DSC distress call and Wi-Fi based AIS with separate antenna. All fixed rigging connections to deck were changed in 2022 and several halyards and sheets have been changed between 2021 and 2023. The Mini is ready to sail coastal regattas or vacation sailing both offshore and coastal sailing.
