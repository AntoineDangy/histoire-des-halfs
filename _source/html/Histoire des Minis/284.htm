1996-98 plan de Welsford, construit par lui même.

In 94, Chris introduced himself to small boat disigner J. Welsford with the words: "I've got no money, but I'd like you design me a boat for mT.
I'm aiming for 1997". Sayer built the cedar and glass boat himself working hour after hours. He estimates he has put more than Nzdollars 60 000, without 1200 hours labour.
