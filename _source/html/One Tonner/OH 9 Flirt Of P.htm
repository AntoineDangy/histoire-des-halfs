2024 April, For Sale Ron Holland 1-ton, 1981, FLIRT OF PAGET, Price: Highest bidder with free trial. Built in New Zealand in 1981 as a light and rigid Grand Prix racer, the Flirt of Paget was groundbreaking at the time with an internal spaceframe that was integrated with the mast's ring frame, shroud and strut. The boat is unique and attracts a lot of attention wherever you sail, if you are interested I recommend that you come and look at her. Sweden's most renowned boat designer Gabriel Heyman has designed the interior, deck layout and sail drawing and it has turned out that it works very well. There is a construction blog on sailing anarchy: https://forums.sailinganarchy.com/index.php?/topic/117372-restoration-ron-holland-ior-racer-flirt-of-paget-2/ She is built in cold-baked KAURI a completely rot-resistant New Zealand wood, two diagonal layers and a horizontal layer on the outside, 20x20 lamelled frames each 120 mm make the hull extremely strong and rigid, the deck is made of plywood and balsa (6, 20, 6 mm) and on top there is biaxial 400 g carpet and NM 276 epoxy. Inside, there is an aluminum ring bulkhead and a tubular structure that connects the grate iron, forestay and backstay with the ring bulkhead, keel and berth fronts, modified to blend in with the boat's interior. This results in an extremely light and strong construction. The hull and deck are painted with Awlgrip paint, below the waterline the hull is impregnated with NM 276, laminated with epoxy and biaxial 400 g carpet before Hempel's light primer and antifouling.
