Backlash, K 7700
à jour au: 1989
1985 Plan Julian Everitt, Design #0103a

 

Not built as One Tonner

 

"She was quite distinctive at the time as having a masthead rig, but with a large sale main (note long boom) and narrow fore triangle. Was RORC Yacht of the Year (1985 or thereabouts).
Definitely the Julian Everitt Backlash. As well as the bulb keel she also had a forward lifting canard - Julian was/is something of an innovator!"
