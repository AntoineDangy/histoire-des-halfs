2018 Avril, For Sale Odiseus VI is an unique boat among the denominated "One Tonner". Built in Astilleros Nauta (Barcelona) using the original design of the naval architect Dubois with the purpose of participating in the 1980 IOR world championship. Completely built in laminated Canadian cedar (4 laters) with an external Samanguila layer. Since July 2008 the boat has remained dry under cover, with the rigging and the appendices unmounted. Negotiable price. Odiseus VI is a very exclusive prototype. The shipyard did not built any more boats of this model, there are a few boats similar, it is an unique boat. The boat is very know and appreciated in the Cantabrian Sea regattas, achieving a great record book. (vous pouvez acheter ce Dubois One Tonner de 1980 situé en Biscaye (Espagne) pour 40.000€. Il s'agit d'un voilier de régate de 11,20m de longueur, photos de l'annonce).
