November, from Facebook For Sale 1981 Judel Vrolijk Admirals Cupper, EUR 49.999,- Espada is a Judel/Vrolik designed boat in composite made for the 81’ Admirals cup for the german team. It is the sistership of “Düsselboot” (later “Outsider). The hull is made of kevlar/balsa sandwich, deck kevlar/foam sandwich and the cockpit from aluminium honey comp. The superstructure is made of kevlar honeycomb. The kiel from antimony hardened lead and the rudder from carbon with titan-shaft. This boat has been continuously maintained/refitted and upgraded during the last years. Hydraulik for backstay, vang and babystay was entirely reviewed in 2017 with new tubes, seals and 4-way chooser ventil. New AGM batteries in 2017 and cabling. New Taktik/Raymarine wireless instruments for wind/speed/depth with 2 solar driven displays in the cockpit in 2022/2023. LED light new (position lights and inside) in 2017. 3 set of sails. And much more older Sails for cruising/training. New G1 Contender Sailcloth Black Aramid in 2018 (like new. sailed 3 times) New Mainsail from Olimpic Sails in Twaron (Carbon) 2019 (like new. sailed 3 times) G2 from North Sails in 2017 - 3Di Race Gold. Mainsail and G3 from Kevlar (Spidertech). Strauss Sails. Still in perfect condition. Completely new security equipment in 2017 with new liferaft ORC 8 Persons with Pack 2 for ORC Races. 3 Spinnakers from light to heavy wind. New ORC certificate in 2019 ( 601,6 GPH , CDL = 9,692 ). 8 Berths in total with new cushions. 17 HP engine from Vetus with Volvo Penta saildrive and folding prop. New carbon/titan rudder in 2015. Raymarine autopilot, Garmin GPS Plotter, Stereo, Tacktick NMEA WIFI. All Halyards, running and trimming ropes in Dyneema/Vectran. Nearly all blocks have been replaced during the last 3 years by new Harken/Kohloff/Ronstan blocks.
