Complete revision of the drive shaft / clutch with new propeller shaft and engine mounts. Autohelm (now Raymarine) lineair drive with new Raymarine Evolution Sail 200 and Raymarine ST6001 Smart pilot display system... No expenses have been spared. The investments in improving the ship and additional equipment are close to 100.000 Euros.
