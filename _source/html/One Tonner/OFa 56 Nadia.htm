2019 For Sale, Nadia IV is a Veteran of 11 Sydney - Hobart races, The Hobart IOR Cup 2015 she placed 6th overall out of 14 in Division 1 after a 1st in the last race; She also won line honours and handicap double in the Sydney - Noumea race. The owner will deliver the boat anywhere on the East Coast of Australia Free of charge as part of the deal. Huge Price Reduction ! These classic Farr Design ex One Tonners are making a comeback! And why not, they offer great performance under sail, with minor changes below also offer excellent space for comfortable cruising.PLUS built by renowned Milner of WA with Kevlar as a critcal part of the hull and deck, its almost bullet proof! Organised club regattas are being held in several locations around Australia including here in Hobart for former IOR racing boats. A wonderful opportunity to compete in a real one design regatta in a family cruiser racer. This boat was a great performer in the hey day of the class, competed successfully under the 1 ton rating rule, sailed in a number of Sydney Hobarts and was always a consistent performer. In addition to the racing options, such as perfect twilight-er, WAGS, and Saturday Club racing, this great boat also has a very spacious saloon, berths for up to 10 persons, good galley with refrigeration etc, and a great cockpit for crew to work in but equally to relax in at anchor. The boat is well powered by a 30hp Yanmar diesel and has a great sail wardrobe, plus cruising items such as electric anchor winch, really nothing to do but jump on and enjoy. We believe the boat offers great value at this price ( how many 40 footers with this level of gear are under $50k?) plus many options for use.
