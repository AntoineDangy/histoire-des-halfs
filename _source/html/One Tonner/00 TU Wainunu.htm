Picture and comment from Facebook 2021 "Aztec. Sail number 2335. Alloy Cole 42 2 tonner built-in 1977. One Syd/Hob race. The previous name was Wainunu V. This boat is owned by a mate of mine. Originally she was a flush decked racer but when he bought her she had a glasshouse dog box! This was stripped off and for several years she was sailed flush decked. The alloy cabin was built over the winter months. All the welding is Mig and was done at the mooring. The whole idea of the cabin was to get headroom over the galley but it has also improved the appearance. He would love to hear from previous owners or crew for some history."
