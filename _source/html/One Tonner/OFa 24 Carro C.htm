The first one was designed by Antonio José Ferrer and built in 1980, 37 foot, sail number BRA 177, was later sold to the Brazilian Navy and renamed Sargaço. This boat raced in the 1980 and 1981 One Ton Cups as Carro Chefe and in the 1983 One Ton Cup as Sargaço.
