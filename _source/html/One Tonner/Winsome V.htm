2018 For Sale, Contessa 38 built in 1973 - A rare opportunity to purchase this classic racing yacht from the 1970s, built by Jeremy Rogers based upon a design by Sparkman and Stephens. One of only six built, she was designed to IOR rules, competing in the 1973 One-Ton cup series. Subsequently converted for cruising, she raced and cruised in Baltic waters and then the Mediterranean for nearly 20 years before being purchased by her current Owner in 1997.

2019 Septembre, nouveau propriétaire Pierre Van Halteren qui va baser le bateau à Carentan, Normandie, France
