2025 Located in New Zealand
February, Global Solo Challenge Website, For Sale, The boat an Open 40 with canting keel and asymetric daggerboards, Winds of Change, is the sistership of Wind of Change, which Viktor Yazikov sailed to fourth place in the 1998 Around Alone race, just behind three 50-foot boats. In Sochi, Russia, Viktor, and Bob Adams built two identical full carbon (including mast and boom ) boats. Victor’s boat completed another round-the-world trip with a Danish owner, and later, a Dutch owner sailed half the way. Unfortunately, he collided with a big drift net, damaged the keel, and had to abandon the ship. The design has been proven successful and fast. When the current owner bought the boat from Bob Adams, she had been laid up for a long time. He purchased the boat in 2015 and installed a new Yanmar 3 cyl. engine. In 2016 did an overhaul during COVID-19, and bought new sails: Main squarehead 2021, Jib 105 % approx, Staysail, and Gennaker all 2022. only the storm jib is original. The 4 person double floor Viking liferaft is from 2015 and has a certificate from 2021. Also, an Eberspacher cabin heater, a watermaker, and a solar panel have been installed. The boat has an EV 100 Raymarine autopilot with electric tiller actuation. Most blocks were replaced in 2022. There is a 4 pers inflatable with 3.5 hp 2-stroke engine with the boat. What is interesting is the location of the canting keel, which is far aft. This makes sailing balanced only with the mainsail possible, which is very convenient when sailing solo (see the boat review). The boat has a reduced wet surface in comparison with the actual class 40, which results in very good light wind performance. The current owner sailed about 25000 Nm. the boat is now in New Zealand. Delivery to Europe can be discussed.
