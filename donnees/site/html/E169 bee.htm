2024 October, Hunter Boats UK HB31, for sale in Pwllheli, North Wales, boasts an overall length of 9.45 metres and a beam of 3.10 metres. Manufactured in 1984, this half-ton cruiser/racer features a Kevlar hull with a lead fin keel. Equipped with a Proctor alloy 3/4 fractional rig and a Harken twin foil furling system. Sail inventory includes a recently purchased Ullman racing main and a variety of other high-quality sails. The boat's deck gear has been updated including Selden removable bowsprit and upgraded winches and clutches. Main saloon offers beamy accommodation with folding table. Safety gear includes a Vespermarine AIS, Garmin Echomap, and various Raymarine wireless instruments.
