Deep Fin keel (16 tonnes ballast). LOA 25.40m Beam 5.98m Draught 4.30m Air Draught 32m Displacement 32Tonnes, ... All Sail made by WitteVrongel in 2001 and has a printed in logo of Peugeot. .... As you would expect in a true racer the interior of this vessel is brought down to its essentials. There is mainly one open area in which a dinette, chart table, a heads compartment with hand basin and ten berths are accommodated. The dinette includes a gas oven and a two burner stove and a sink with an electrical and manual water supply. Due to the open plan of the interior it s unproblematic to adjust the interior to your wishes. A vessel with a great history and good potential for the future. Very suitable for the more adventurous charter activities. Will need work carried out on the hydraulic winch system but then any boat like this is going to require expenditure. The world class solo racers Michel Desjoyeaux & Roland Jourdain both sailed with the immortal Eric Tabarly on this yacht in the 1985 Whitbread. It was the first time for both of them they had crossed the Equator !
