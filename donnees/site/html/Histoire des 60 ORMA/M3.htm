Un jour le bateau a disparu.....les propriétaires aussi.... il paraîtrait qu'ils naviguaient vers les Antilles...."

December, received from Sanne Van Der Duin "Hi I’ve taken it upon me to restore and old French cata for the last two summers. It was quite an ordeal, 10 meter tear along starboard hull keel (vacuum infuse mono coque epoxy glass carbon and core), New bows, New sterns, New bulkheads. We hope to have her floating in march next year and are currently reinstalling all systems. Very happily because a heavy labor is in the past. The whole project was a lot of work much more than we anticipated and we hope to use the boat in French pacific for edutainment/cultural exchange type work letting local youths explore and work alongside western (USA/eU/aus) youths" and new name ship,
