2024 February, Farr 30 Cruiser Racer - In Pender Harbour - $12,300 (Hospital Bay), This Farr 920 is one of Farr's first "big boat" designs. A great racer/weekender that is ready for anything. October 2023 survey available! Aft are two large berths. A midships galley and nav station lead to a comfortable salon area, and the racing forepeak includes a private head and sail storage. Headroom is about 5'10" over the galley with Force 10 single-burner propane stove. On deck all lines lead aft to the cockpit for easy shorthanding. Most lines are dyneema. The fractional rig has swept spreaders, small jibs, and NO running backstays - easy and very modern. Power is from a Tohatsu 4-stroke 6hp outboard, mounted on a track to keep it mostly inside the boat. This keeps the moorage bill down to a 30 foot slip. Specs: LOA 30.25 Beam 10.0 Draft 5.25 Disp 4894 lbs, Construction: fiberglass with local reinforcement in carbon fiber. Hull is solid (not cored), deck is balsa cored.
