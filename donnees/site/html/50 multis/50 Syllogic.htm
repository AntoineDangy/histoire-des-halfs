2015 4 November, BGYB is delighted to announce that we have been appointed Central Agent for the Multi 50 racing trimaran TRILOGIC: "Launched in June 2003 as FenetreA Cardinal,(Wrong!!) this 50 ft racing trimaran was renamed Trilogic in 2013 by her current owner. The work of naval architects Joubert-Nivelt and Marc Lombard, skipper Eric Brunel sailed some 25,000 miles on Trilogic during her early years. Then FenetreA-Cardinal, she was sold in 2008 to JB Cunin who registered her under a French flag, and appointed Erwan Le Roux as skipper. Over a period of 5 years, he achieved a great deal of success in the major multihull races. In 2013, she was sold to Italian skipper Crasta Gavion and renamed TRILOGIC".
