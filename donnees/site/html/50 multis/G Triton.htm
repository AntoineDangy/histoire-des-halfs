Native Rainbow

1999 March, "NEMA" (New England Multihull Association,: "If you see a graceful apparition out of Star Wars with a 65' mast go by in Maine at twenty knots or more, she’s probably Brad Johansen’s Native Rainbow. Rainbow (ex Promocean) is a fourteen year old 40' tri designed by Adrian Thompson and built by Tom Foley in Devon, England to race on the European Formula 40 race circuit. Johansen, an accomplished builder and former Pact 95 team member, has spent 3 years refurbishing her. She’ll likely be scratch boat in any race she enters."
