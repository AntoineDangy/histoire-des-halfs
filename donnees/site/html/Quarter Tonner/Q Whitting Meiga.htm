Picture and info from Dutch QT Class, Facebook

2020 August, From Facebook, "Nouvelle vie pour le MEIGA XVI comme day-sailer. En 1977, nous avons construit cette IOR 1/4 ton à l'image et à la ressemblance du révolutionnaire Magic Bus qui avait gagné le Mondial de Quarter Ton en 76. Son designer Paul Whiting de Nouvelle-Zélande était un designer passionné et constructeur de bateaux de régate et comprenait comme personne les structures ultra légères en bois. En 1978, de la main de Rodrigo Andrade, Tato Alonso, Javier Santander et Alfredo Lagos Sr, le MEIGA a remporté la régate Rias basses. Après plusieurs années à sec ici sur le chantier naval et après une restauration profonde, il naviguera à nouveau comme daysailer"




2024 March, for sale 2020 Lagos Quarter Ton Day Sailer, This pure Day Sailer designed by New Zealander Paul Whiting is the essence of sailing, it is entirely made of African mahogany laminated in three layers and has more than 10 layers of varnish for protection. You don't have to do anything to do with it and it's cruised for ease of use. It has a new 10 hp diesel inboard engine installed, it was set to 0 of the boat in 2020. It was built for the owners of the Lagos Shipyard in Bouzas and they owned it until 2020. That same year a total renovation was carried out, this one as good as new.
