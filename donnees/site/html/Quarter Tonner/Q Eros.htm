1986(?) Italian VI classe IOR championship in Ravenna: ?e/??,

Pictures and comment recieved from Manlio Gesuè (2020), "The designer, Mino Simeone, was a genius! Eros looked like a Stradivarius violin, and had some nice solutions never seen on class VI (for example a flush-mounted spotlight on the nose of the bow pointed upwards to look at the tell-tails of the genoa). He was one of the first (on Cuordileone with whom we lost the One Ton Cup 80 in Naples due to an early departure) to mount the mainsail travel to the stern of the rudder."
