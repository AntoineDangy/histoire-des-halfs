1987 plan Tony Castro, MG26 modified

Seahorse QTC 87: "McDonald's is a MG 26, that is she is made out of the same moulds and thu has the same hull and deck shape as the standard boat. Built by Stephen Etheridge, her construction is totally different though as she is of the 'exotic family'. The result is of course less weight particularly in the ends and a boat that is much easier to get into rating trim. Her rated displacement is nearly 300kgs less for the same sail area as the standard boats and the corresponding ratios stand out in the MG fleet."
