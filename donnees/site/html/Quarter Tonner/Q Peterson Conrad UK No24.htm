"Here winning the 2016 Port Boulgas Keelboat Regatta. Not bad for a 1978 design. Skippered by multi national champion ( those and other classes) Mr Mikhail Kopanov from Yacht Club "Port Bourgas". Usually up to 15 boats of same monotype( out of 30) are visible on the start line for each of the local race events . Thank your post and tag. In Bulgaria, we are valuing the build as a classic and apart from the pictured boat named "Uk Sailmakers" and helmed by Mr Kopanov, there are numerous other owners and crews who invest a great amount of time and effort in maintaining a piece of this very competitive "specimen" of sailing engineering. Racing the Conrad 760 in Bulgaria is a matter of prestige and recently few other restoration projects have begun to increase the above-mentioned number. For the sake of our Conrad 760 society, we`d hope if we can reach you should we have any construction or sourcing issues in case you would be able to address any queries of the kind", Picture and comment from Peterson Design Facebook 2018,
