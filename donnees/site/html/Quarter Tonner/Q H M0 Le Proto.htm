2019 From Dutch QT Class Facebook, Esa Vilmusenaho "My father bought the 1977 winner Manzanita at autum 1977. WB sails entrepeneurs Mikko Brummer and Rudi Biaudet were helping the owner in sales. Mikko and Rudi were Flying Dutchman sailors and excellent sail makers. Manzanita was racing at lake Päijänne and reached many first prices at it’s class. Päijännepurjehdus (Päijänne sail race) has most likely been the biggest lake side sail race. 265 boats starting together. Manzanita was third at it’s best. ”Washig” many bigger boats." "Manzanita is now retired and is waiting for complete refit at my boat yard at Middle Finland"
