February, from Facebook, 'Adrenaline' is a world class Formula 40 racing trimaran built by the Gougeon brothers, the famous yacht designers and builders and the creators of West System marine epoxy and composites. Built in 1987 at a cost of US$581,000 (in 2020 $) to compete in the European Formula 40 multihull racing class series. This is a very unique and cutting edge racing boat. 40ft long by 40 feet wide with a 62 foot rotating mast, carbon boom and articulating amas (utilizing custom Harken titanium hinges). She was one of the fastest sailboats in the world, and still easily achieves over 20 knots. Built from Douglas fir, Carbon fiber and West System epoxy. Sail inventory includes excellent condition Doyle carbon mainsail, Doyle carbon jib, new Doyle spinnaker used only 3-4 times and Doyle code zero screecher. Trampolines are in excellent condition and boat has 8x self tailing large Harken winches and several smaller winches. Many spares including dagger boards and rudders. Cradles and dollies for hardstand included. This boat presents a great opportunity for the right buyer. Raced in Phuket regional regattas and Phuket Yacht Club events for the last 6 years, health issues force a quick sale. Asking only US$20k!
