2016 December, For Sale Year: 2004 Length: 86' Located In: La Ciotat, France Hull Material: Composite Current Price: EUR  1,500,000 (£ 1,278,332)  Reichel/Pugh Yachts are pleased to announce that the 86' Super Maxi Racing Yacht Morning Glory is now for sale.  Morning Glory, the Record Breaking Canting Keel Super Maxi, built by McConaghy Boats, has had a strong presence in the Mediterranean Maxi racing scene since it was launched in 2004. The yacht has an enviable trophy list that amongst many wins and first to finish placings includes:  Winner of the 2007 Maxi Worlds, Racing Division  Winner of the 2006 Middle Sea Race  1st to Finish and New Course Record in the 2005 Transpac Race  1st to Finish and New Course Record in the 2004 Newport to Bermuda Race  Morning Glory is a high performance racing yacht that has been professionally maintained and has a proven offshore and inshore track record. This Record Breaking Super Maxi is a turn key solution that will allow her new owner to compete at the highest level in any of the major regattas around the world.  Drawings of the deck arrangements & interior, additional information and photographs are available from our office upon request.
