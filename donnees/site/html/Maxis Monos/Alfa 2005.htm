From Facebook: "Extensive modifications made for the Sydney-Hobart race 2019: After acquiring 100ft Reichel-Pugh Esmit Europa (ex. Alfa Romeo) and her return to Australia, she finished second in the 2018 Rolex Sydney Hobart Yacht Race and inspired the Black Jack Team to look for more performance. The Reichel/Pugh team knew this had to come from a longer, fuller bow section, adding significant volume up forward for power reaching and running offshore. Therefore, 1 meter was cut from the stern to facilitate the construction of a new bow. Additionally, the transom width was extended at the deck level and a longer bowsprit was built to improve the flying sails projection and sheeting efficiency with the mainsail.
