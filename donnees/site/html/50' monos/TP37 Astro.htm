2012 Octobre, For Sale - A Vendre, TP 52 - 2006 Price: €350,000 (Tax Not Paid), Location: Triest, Italy, "Sonic Astro" is a fourth generation TP52. She was designed in 2006 after extensive towing-tank testing and with the original TP52 ideas in mind; to be extremely fast around the cans but to race safely offshore. In her two MedCup seasons she raced without incident and showed good speeds inshore and offshore. Due to her very high spec carbon prepreg construction she started her second life as an IRC race yacht without problems and won almost all the races she has done since. She is now for sale freshly painted with new sails this year and offers her new owners lots of potential for a very succesful IRC campaign.
