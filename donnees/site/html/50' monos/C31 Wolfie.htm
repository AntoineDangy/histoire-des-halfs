WOLFIES TOY, BEL 1197
2007 For Sale, LOA: 15.23 m, Beam: 4.87, Draught: 3.05 m, Design: Georg Nissen, Hull: aluminium, Launch: 1995, Mast: 20,80 m, SA upwind: 150 m², SA downwind: 250 m² , Wolfies Toy an open 50 foot sailboat will be sailed by Veronique Dierickx Visschers and Bartel van Holsbeeck. The boat was built by Wolfgang Quix, an experienced German sailor. Construction started in 1993 with the purpose of entering BOC 1994. The boat participated in the Europe1Star 1996, the Transat Quebec-St. Malo 1996, RB and I 1998 and ARC 2000. Wolfgang Quix undertook an expedition voyage to the Hudson Bay and sailed to Spitzbergen as far as 80 deg. N. The ship is made from aluminium with a carbon mast. Some alterations were made to make the interior more comfortable.
