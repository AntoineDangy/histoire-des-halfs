Décembre, For Sale AMAALTA, Builder: CHANTIER NAVAL POUVREAU, France, Designer: PINA FARINA / André Mauric, Flag of Registry: Other, Hull Shape: Monohull, Dimensions: LOA: 26.10 m, Beam: 4.60 m, Minimum Draft: 3.37 m, Maximum Draft: 3.37 m, Displacement: 27000 kgs, year: 1981, Length: 86', Located In: Sète, France, Hull Material: Aluminum, Current Price: EUR  290,000 Tax Paid (£ 246,996). Designed and developed by André Mauric for Jacky Setton in 1981, AMAALTA, baptized PIONEER at her launch, is one of many yachts owned by this famous connoisseur, known for always being at the avant-garde of yachting. Since the beginning she has been luxuriously decorated for cruising. AMAALTA was built using the same plans as KRITER VIII, commissioned by Michel Malinovski for long distance races, who won the Route du Rhum in 1982 in the Monohull Class. She has also recently undergone a complete refit and offers every possible comfort, in particular a very large Master Cabin.
