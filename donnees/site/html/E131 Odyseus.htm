Luzern, NOR 7449

2021 15 July, HalftonclassEurop website, For Sale BANNER 1/2 TON 1985 - 17.000 EUR, Luzern (NOR 7449) is a shipyard-built cruiser version of the Banner 1/2 ton. The boat has been corrected for the latest rule changes (IOR version 3). The boat has a moderate displacement (calculated IOR weight 2771 kg) and optimal sailing guidance for LYS, which guarantees a good all-round boat. Both hull and deck are hand-laid sandwich construction, where all the exotic materials such as kevlar, carbon fiber and R-glass are used in connection with special polyester. Lead keel according to NACA profile with embedded steel construction and at the same time is the engine foundation. As can be seen in the pictures, the boat is quite spartan, but works well for regatta sailing.
