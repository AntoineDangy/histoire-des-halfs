2024 December "Seahorse", ForSale Figaro Beneteau 3 'Castor'. Price 199,000 US Dollars USD. Located in California. The Figaro Beneteau 3 has the distinction of being the first, production, foiling one design monohull. A result of the collaboration between VPLP and Beneteau, the Figaro 3 improves on its predecessor with foils that increase lift and reduce leeway, an optimised slender and deeper keel, no need for water ballast, a more extensive sail plan, which all adds up to a significant 15% increase in performance. 'Castor' has been optimised and tailor made for short-handed offshore racing with the addition of extra performance enhancing features including:  2 metre carbon bowsprit by Courouble Design & Engineering, Lightweight Axxon mast, Colligo Dux standing rigging, McConaghy carbon stanchions, pushpits, and pulpits, Carbon tiller and custom carbon spinlock tiller extension, Carbon bunks / stack racks, The boat (previously known as “A Fond La Girafon”) has been true to its racing pedigree, winning Line Honors in the 2018 Pacific Cup and competing in Transpac 2019. More recently she has been raced double-handed close to her home in Long Beach, California  in the SoCal 300, and Santa Barbara to King Harbor Race. ‘Castor’ comes with an extensive sail inventory, including brand new main sail, J1/2, and A2 (Incidence, 2023). This a rare opportunity to acquire a highly specified boat in this Pacific region.  ‘Castor’ has been impeccably maintained and presents race ready for ocean races such as Transpac25 and other adventures. LOA: 10.89m Beam: 3.48m Draft: 2.5m Material: Foam sandwich, fibreglass and polyester infused resin Designer: Beneteau/ VPLP Launched: 2018
