There are several singlehanded events early in the season and then a couple more during the summer months before the mighty Solitaire du Figaro in late August and September. Come and try this exceptional class of strict One Design offshore racing. The competition standard is high, the circuit is exciting and the friendships are for life.... Figaro Number 38 is available for charter for part or all of the season.
