Located in La Trinite, France, Eora is a fantastic example of the latest design from Marc Lombard, a Lift V2 Class 40, built with Glass Epoxy Sandwich by Innovation Composites and launched in 2021. In her short career in 2022, she placed 6 th in the Normandy Channel Race while the Lift V2 design is at the top of the game with results such as: 2022 Route du Rhum 1 st and 3 rd , 2022 Drheam Cup 2 nd and 2022 Normandy Channel Race 2 nd .
