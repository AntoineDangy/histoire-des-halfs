Vendée Globe 2020,

 

2019, Rich Wilson (Concurent du Vendée Globe 2016) a créé le Collegiate Offshore Sailing Circuit avec 10 Figaros 2: "As Vendée Globe approaches (I have hotel reservations for the start but no boat nor entry!), I thought you might be interested in our next project. We have formed the Collegiate Offshore Sailing Circuit, Inc. (see www.cosc-usa.org ) with the goal to expand the pool of offshore capable mariners in the USA by bringing true offshore sailing to college sailing teams (which typically only sail dinghies). When in France around my two Vendee Globes (2008-9 & 2016-7) we were amazed about how much offshore sailing, racing, cruising there was. We also noticed that the fundamental building block boat for the great French ocean sailors, the Figaro 2, was transitioning to the Figaro 3. Thus we imagined raising money in the US to buy and ship a group of these boats to the US for deployment to colleges in the USA. We visited the Maritime/Service Academies, plus a half dozen private colleges, and received great encouragement. We took that to potential donors and have raised the funds to buy 10 Figaro 2s and bring them here. 6 are now deployed to colleges; we expect the other 4 to be deployed in the spring."
Voici la liste des 10 Figaros 2: 7 Anne, 19 Vicitan, 27 Raga, 43 Abu, 45 John & Dorothy, 50 Monin, 56 Redshift, 66 Williwaw, 83 Benita, 96 Spirit,
