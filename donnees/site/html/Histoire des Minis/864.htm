UAE 864, Abu Dhabi,
à jour au: février 2015
2015 plan G Yacht Design, RG650 built by Riotecna in Argentina

Information and picture from RG650 Europe blog:


2014 29 novembre, Butti Al Mheiri arrived in Valencia to see for the first time his brand new RG 650 #864.

2015 February, 864 will be in Abu Dhabi until this time next year as he promotes the class and his campaign in his home waters. Once he has had a year of sailing he will bring the boat back to the EU where he will be a familiar face at various events in the build up to MT 17.
