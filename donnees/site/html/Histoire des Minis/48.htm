2014 April, new owner: Stefano Blason, 24 years old from Monfalcone, Italy.

2015 25 février, received mail from Stefano Blason: " In april 2014 I bought "Peau d'Orange" from Claudio Serra in Porto Torres, Sardinia dreaming adventures. I lived sailing across the Italy for about four months. First sailing Porto Torres-Naples, Naples-Otranto, Otranto-Vieste.  The boat remained in Vieste until February 2015 due to job call. With my sailing partner Polo Manuel before start we check the keel and the main pin was cracked. Carried the kell in the car we drive back and fix it. Then down again.  In a particular weather window we start the last 260nm to Monfalcone pushing the old boat at maximum for avoid the incoming Bora but, in the last 40nm, we get approx 40 to 60kn. Fortunately we like sail and after 60hrs we reach home safe and wet. The boat now is under refit because she is a part of history but we are awaiting sponsors for someting newer.."
