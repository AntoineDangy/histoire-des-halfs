From Tony Castro Design Facebook 2021, "Yeoman XXV - this raceboat was beautifully built with extremely light 3mm topsides plating, glued and riveted. A masterpiece of aluminium construction. The layout and systems were state-of-art with lots of interesting innovations. Biil Edgerton worked tirelessly to get the boat prepared and to perform.
The design was very similar to my 43 footer "Izatanotherpurla" that was so successful when raced in the right hands. Unfortunately, the year Yeoman 25 was launched, the competition was already full of professional helmsman and paid crews, which a bunch of very talented amateurs could not compete with. The boat did have some notable successes nevertheless and won the Round the Island of Wight race in a year that had more than 2000 entries. It later became BLADE and in the hands a Canadian crew and helmed by the great Pol Ricard Jensen, won most races it entered including Miami-Nassau race.."
