2019 Picture from Northern and Southern Ireland racing yachts past and present Facebook, Stephen Boyd: "I did a Round Ireland and a Fastnet race on her in 1997 & 1998 when she was owned by Gary and Susan Horgan. She was owned by the late Richard Rohu in the late 80s early 90s he also did a round Ireland on her, she had graduated brown stripes back then. I went to school with Richard’s daughter Hilary. The Kingston Family of Kinsale Boatyard gave her her current livery who I worked for whilst a student. With the help of the custodian of the Holman & Pye archive I did my final year engineering dissertation looking at the effect of shroud pre-tension on the stresses in the mast tube using early finite element analysis. It seems this boat has been a part of my life for a long time!",
