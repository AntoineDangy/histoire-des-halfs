Swan 37, plan Stephens, Builder:  Nautor, from 1970 to 1974, 59 builts.

LOA:  36.50' / 11.13m LWL:  27.25' / 8.31m, Beam:  10.67' / 3.25m Listed SA:  598 ft2 / 55.55 m2, Draft (max.)  6.10' / 1.86m Draft (min.)
Disp.  15400 lbs./ 6985 kgs. Ballast:  7300 lbs. / 3311 kgs. SA/Disp.:  15.51 Bal./Disp.:  47.40% Disp./Len.:  339.76

S&S #2035. Sold also as PALMER JOHNSON 37 (S&S #2035.1). Intended for racing at the IOR 1 ton level at the time.
A number of other custom boats (from other builders) were built to this same design. Some delivered with a taller rig.(See SWAN 37 TM)
