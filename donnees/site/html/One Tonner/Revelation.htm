Gumboot IV, KA

2021 March, For Sale, Dubois 40 Gumboot IV (1 Tonner / Admirals Cup), Price AU $39,900, Length 40' 0" - 12.20m, Vessel Location: Martin Box Marine Fremantle WA Launch Year 1983; Hull Material Fibreglass/GRP; Gumboot IV is a well sailed and well looked after 40 foot racer / cruiser originally built for the Admirals Cup in 1983. Lovingly maintained throughout her life, she has had many dollars spent on upgrades such as a new Yanmar diesel motor in 2012 and a full new set of Raymarine electronics to the tune of over $15,000. A huge sail inventory includes number 1,2 and 3 headsails with storm jib and tri-sail, a new mainsail in 2017 and two spinnakers and well as a Code 0. Capable of sleeping 10 people she is still competitive in both inshore and offshore racing and has sailed in iconic races including the Sydney to Hobart. A Highfield tender is included as well as a life raft. She was sand blasted in 2017 back to the primer and now has a smooth bottom ready for racing. This is your chance to own a classic IOR 1 tonner and for a very low price considering the electronics, sail inventory and new engine.
