May, For sale, located in Cork, 27 000 Euros, Rob Humphries custom design # 54 One Tonner performance sailing yacht that will put in a performance to leave many newer builds astern. Designed specifically for round the cans and Irish Sea performance for fast fetching and reaching. Her performance is well recorded with many wins on the record. Now with the advantage of age allowance. Built by Stephen Etheridge who built many of the Humphries custom & specials. GRP with modified Welbourne keel. Triple spreader mast with runners and rod rigging. Tiller steering with two berths and six cots below. Yanmar 3GM diesel with two blade folding prop and replacement shaft. Here's a yacht of provenance with plenty of punch TCC 1.013 and Regatta Ready.
