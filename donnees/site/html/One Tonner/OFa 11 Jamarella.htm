Hitchhiker III stern was altered in response to the 1989 IOR rule changes, but Hitchhiker III didn't make the Australian team, and was instead chartered towards the Irish Team but was only a shadow of her former self. She finished in 32nd place, but was the best placed of the lowly 13th placed Irish team."
