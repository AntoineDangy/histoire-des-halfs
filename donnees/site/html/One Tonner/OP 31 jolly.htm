Jolly Olly IV, CND 19875
Last update: 2024
1974 Plan Peterson, Peterson 35 #1, built by Don Martin at Cooper yachts in Surry B.C.

Jolly Olly IV was the first (boat #1) from which the mould was made for all other Peterson 35s where made

 

1982 Picture from Royal Vancouver Yacht Club,



2022 12 August, For Sale Peterson 35 - $28,000 (Squamish), Jolly Olly IV was the original Peterson 35. 40 others came off the mold of this one. It is still in remarkably excellent condition as it was owned by the original skipper until recently. This boat has a full inventory of sails including 2 mains,many jibs and genoas and 5 spinnakers. The 18hp Yanmar diesel in good running condition. Well appointed inside with working gimballed stove, lots of teak, VHF, sounder, stereo, tools, anchor, marine head, foul weather gear and life jackets. Rigging is in good condition, hydraulic backstay. Great racing history, won tons of regattas and still a fast boat. Moorage not necessarily transferable.
