2015 Pictures from One Ton Class Facebook,

2017 February seen on Australian Racing list: owner: Roy Brough, "Once More Dear Friends was the first yacht built by John McConaghy, to a design of Ed Dubois for Peter Kurts. As to her racing pedigree OMDF represented Australia at the 1983 Admirals Cup and completed thirteen Hobarts coming second twice. Although a cruising vessel now, she did make the journey from Newcastle to Geelong for the Scandia Regatta in 2006 to once more participate in an arena she knows only too well."
