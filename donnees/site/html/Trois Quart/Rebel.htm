Schuss, K 4052
Last Update: June 2017
1985 plan Humphreys, MGRS34, Longueur: 10.24 m, Largeur: 3.52 m, Tirant eau: 1.91, Tirant air: 12.75

 

1986 Launched and raced in Plymouth.

1989 Schuss was sold to a company in the Solent and renamed “Design Star” but chartered racing yachts have a very hard time in the Solent.
Design Star, GBR 4052

From Facebook 2023 "I did a couple of Hamble River Winter Series races on an RS34 called Design Star when I was a student in the early '90s. Lovely machine, but it knew how to broach!"

1993 New owner, Nigel Astbury-Rollason, new home port: Plymouth and new name,
Rebel, GBR 4052

2001 Fastnet Race: 150e/190 finishers, IRC TCC: 0.972, Nigel ASTBURY-ROLLASON

2003 10 August, Fastnet Race, elapsed time 5d03h52', 95e/234 IRC Overall, Nigel ASTBURY-ROLLASON

2005 Fastnet Race: 46e/254 finishers elapsed time 5d00h36', IRC TCC: 0.974, Royal Western Yacht Club
Photo extraite du site de la Semaine du Golfe 2017,

2010 Round Mann Race (Isle de Man): record holder for the PY Class of 10 hours 4 minutes

2014 May, Nigel Astbury-Rollason: " I purchased a clapped out racing yacht and after six long weekends of hard work fixing almost everything, she was sailed back to Plymouth and her mooring at Weir-Quay Boatyard.
We have competed in 4 Fastnet Races and acheived creditable finishes. We use Rebel for family cruising and racing in offshore events……..no round the cans stuff……..We did plenty of that racing Merlin-Rockets for many years (still a great dinghy to sail) In fact sailing Rebel is just like sailing a Flippin’ Big Merlin-Rocket ……….with running back stays !!"

2015 On port of Plymouth Sailing Association list

"REBEL has won the Yealm to Trebeurden race three times"
