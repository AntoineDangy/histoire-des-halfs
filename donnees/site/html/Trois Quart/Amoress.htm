Amoress , built by Lennart Minnegard & Arne Johnsson at Spillersboda Vara, Stockholm for Bo Andersson has a outstanding racing pedigree, participating in countless regattas and was, at the time the boat to beat, particularly upwind in heavy weather. Maintained in exceptional original condition Amoress is of cold moulded mahogany construction, bright finished inside and out, clearly showing her fine construction.
