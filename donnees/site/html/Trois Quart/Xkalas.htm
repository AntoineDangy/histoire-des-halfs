2018 September, received from Martin Kautz: "End of 2016 I bought an X-102 in Stockholm. It's #150 (built in 1986), previously named "Kalas" (D 150), then "Pryde" (D 150). In 2002 the boat got sold to the swedisch sailor Göran Säwensten who renamed it to "Xäwen" and changed the sail number to SWE 150. Today the boat is located in Rostock, Germany (in fact 100 meters away from the berth of Speedsailing and their famous VOR60 cuppers) and got again renamed to "F.O.X." which is an abbrevation for "Five On X". We also changed the number of the new mainsail to GER 150."
