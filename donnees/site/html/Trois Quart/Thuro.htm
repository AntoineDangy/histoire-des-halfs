For Sale SY „Gingang“ GER 2314 is a gentlemen's sailor who has undergone regular maintenance and refit throughout the years. She is ready to go for a weekend or holiday cruise, or just on Sunday afternoons for a gentle cruise. Thurö boats have been handcrafted by the Danish Walsted shipyard and were renowned to be the Danish Swan in those days. Her classical shapes are head-turning in every marina. The engine is renewed, such as sails, teak deck and so much more. Just get on board and enjoy.
