2018 April, For Sale Custom Humphreys 3/4 Ton, Accommodations The ¾ Tonner offers spacious cabin with good headroom. Can sleep up to eight, including four permanent berths and up to four pipe cots ( one fitted and 3 provided loose). This is a cruiser/racer and the internal layout reflects this. The accommodation plan is as follows starting in the bow: Forepeak with stowage for sails and general equipment. Heads compartment with Lavac sea toilet and wash basin. Main saloon has setee berths port and starboard with lee cloths attached. Behind saloon berths is stowage. Galley on starboard side of companionway comprises of Gimballed single burner gas stove with oven and grill, Stainless steel sink with pressurised or manual water, plentiful storage for cutlery, pots and pans and provisions. On port side opposite galley is chart table with stowage and aft of this on both sides are quarter berths with lee cloths. Sails and Spars Rig by Proctor new in 1995. Keel stepped mast with twin in line spreaders. Slab reefing main boom with rod kicker. Twin luff headfoil and spinnaker pole. Stainless steel rod standing rigging, renewed September 2011.
