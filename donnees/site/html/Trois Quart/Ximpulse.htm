2022 August, X YACHT 3/4 TONNER FOR SALE, Ramsgate, Kent, United Kingdom, £15,995 GBP, This X-Yachts built this 3/4 Tonner in 1984 is in good condition for her age. The 3/4 Tonner is a fast racer, but also makes a great fast cruising yacht. This yacht offers 10 berths in two cabins with 6ft headroom. She had a new Northrop Mainsail and Genoa in 2019.  She has just been antifouled and engine serviced by the owner ready for the current season. the current owner has owned the yacht for the past 25 years and is selling as he is moving up to a larger yacht. Owner is open to reasonable offers for a quick sale. A 2010 survey report is available upon request. Built by X-Yachts to a Niels-Jeppsen design in 1984, hull number 21. Cream coloured GRP form sandwich hull and superstructure with moulded non-slip decks. Galvanised steel internal frame connects the keel, mast step and engine mountings. Cast iron fin keel. Self-draining cockpit with tiller steering to spade hung Kevlar reinforced GRP rudder and tapered alloy stock.
