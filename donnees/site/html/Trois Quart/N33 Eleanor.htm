2017 February, For Sale Fast, Ron Holland-designed Sloop - very well equipped! € 27 500, This is a boat for sailors! Designed by Ron Holland for the Fastnet Race in the English Channel, the Camper-Nicholson 33 is extremely well built, and can still club-race competitively or cruise comfortably. Eleanor Rigby was club raced in Vancouver, BC in the '80's and 90's and cruised mostly since. Her hull was completely refinished in 1986 (faired with foamglas, primed with Awlquik, and finished with Awlgrip). Her Yanmar 2GM20FV diesel was new in 1994, along with a new 16-1/4" Maxprop. Her interior was completely re-done in 1986 by Napier Custom Yachts in Vancouver, BC, including insulation. Cabin heat (SIG Marine LPG bulkhead heater) was just added in 2016. New electrical panel (2005), new battery charger and new batteries (2009), new Pryde full-batten mainsail and lazyjacks and Pryde 150% genoa (2014), new North gennaker with sleeve (2015), and new internal halyards (2006) are just some of the many recently-added components. The boat was thoroughly surveyed in 2015 with an estimated replacement value of $209,500 (copy on file in our office). Her Everett moorage is readily transferable to a new owner.
