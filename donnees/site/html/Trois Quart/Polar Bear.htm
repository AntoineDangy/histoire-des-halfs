Polar Bear, K ????
Last update: 2023
1973 Plan Holman & Pye, built by McGruer & Co No649, Scotland

1973 Owner, John Clothier

2023 August, From Facebook Douglas Peniston "I own Polar Bear, another McGruer built 3/4 ton (649/1973). We have raced her as a Classic and now temporarily moored at Woolverstone before cruising North and hopefully round britain (perhaps via Clynder to pay homage to her builders) next year. Great craftsmanship, now maintained by another great craftsman, Nick Gates in Emsworth".

2024 January, comment from Facebook Douglas Peniston "Here’s my boat Polar Bear - Holman & Pye cold moulded one off from 1974 on which the UFO34 was based. Currently in the North East waiting for spring, en route to the Scottish West coast."
